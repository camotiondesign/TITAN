# Tariq ProPharmace Shorts — Batch 2 (2026-08-11)

**Source:** cuts from Tariq Muhammad's ProPharmace Fable Talk sitting in `~/Downloads/Tariq Vids/`.
**Batch:** 26 shorts covering #1, #3–#6, #8, #10–#29 (gaps #2, #7, #9 were handled in the earlier batch at `tariq-propharmace-shorts-titles.md`).
**Purpose:** each short is a self-contained cut. Titles are curiosity-driven so viewers tap through to the full talk.

Every title stays under 60 characters where possible, uses a curiosity gap, and reads like a human wrote it — not marketing copy. British English throughout, no em dashes, no competitor names on the tin.

**A note on the transcripts:** Whisper base.en did the lift. Light punctuation cleanup only. A few obvious mishears fixed in place: "Titon"/"Tyson" → "Titan", "Bitter" → "Beta" (Tariq's dad using the Urdu/Hindi term for son), product names capitalised. Where a clip is genuinely a sub-cut of a longer one already listed, the titles differentiate on angle.
