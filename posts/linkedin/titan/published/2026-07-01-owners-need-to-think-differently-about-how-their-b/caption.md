Owners need to think differently about how their businesses run.

Dispensing volume is no longer enough on its own, especially with funding pressure, government changes and more services moving into community pharmacy.

Geoff Thomas is a good example of what that next stage looks like.

When we last spoke to Geoff, his two pharmacies in Newport, South Wales had grown from 2,500 items a month to 40,000.

But what stood out was not just the volume.

It was what he was building around it.

✅ Three consultation rooms already in use
✅ Two more planned at Malpas
✅ Clinics expanding into flu, smoking cessation and contraception
✅ A clear focus on freeing up pharmacists to spend more time with patients

At TitanUp 26, Geoff saw what is coming next across Titan PMR and Titanverse.

For someone already pushing hard on services, the reaction says a lot.

Because once a pharmacy has built the volume, the next challenge is building the model around it.
