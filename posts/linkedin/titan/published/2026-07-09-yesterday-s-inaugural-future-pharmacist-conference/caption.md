Yesterday’s inaugural Future Pharmacist Conference was fire.

And not just because @Central Hall Westminster was boiling. 😂🥵

It was the reaction in the room.

A room full of future pharmacists, many of them waiting on their registration results, walked into that day with real energy about what community pharmacy is becoming.

And it showed.

Every time there was a break in the programme, the Titan stand was rammed.

The question that kept coming up was simple:

“The PMR my pharmacy runs on looks really old. Why is Titan different?”

Fair question.

Titan was the first PMR to go paperless.
The first to introduce barcode scanning.
The first to introduce AI to move the pharmacist away from the bench.

But the bigger point is this:

A pharmacist’s time is not best spent stuck behind outdated systems.

It is best spent on clinical care, patient conversations and building the kind of pharmacy business that can actually thrive.
