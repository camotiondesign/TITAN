Private pharmacy services come with huge potential.

But they also move more responsibility onto the pharmacy.

That’s the tension Ghulam Haydar will be unpacking at TitanUp 26.

When you design and deliver your own services, governance cannot be an afterthought. It has to run through how the service is built, delivered, recorded and reviewed.

Ghulam’s motto is simple:

Protect patients. Protect yourself. Protect your business.

And that is exactly where this session lands.

What do private services actually require if you want to scale them safely?

✅ Is the service clinically safe?
✅ Is the team trained to deliver it?
✅ Can the workflow stand up to pressure?
✅ Is the digital system built for the risk?

Ghulam will show how @TITANVERSE helps build those expectations into the everyday delivery of pharmacy services.

So growth does not depend on crossed fingers, scattered records or systems that were never built for the job.
