Today, 7 in 10 prescriptions clear a clinical check without a pharmacist ever touching them. Soon it'll be...

9 in 10.

Titan AI was already doing real work before this. The models were good. But they weren't agentic, and that is the change.

Level 5 is a different build: many specialist models running in sequence, each handling a specific part of the check. Built and trained in-house, run on Titan's own infrastructure, checked against the BNF and against real community pharmacy data. Not a chatbot bolted onto a dispensary.

That leap took real investment. An AI team rebuilt around a professor of AI, a clinical team training the models through tens of thousands of prescriptions, and the legal work to align with how the MHRA classifies a medical device.

Level 5 frees the pharmacist from the routine majority, so their time goes to the patient in front of them, where it counts.
