NHS prescription tracking notifications are due to begin rolling out for TITAN PMR customers during May 2026.

Patients using the NHS App, with notifications enabled, will be able to receive updates when their medicines are:

✅ Ready to Collect
✅ Part ready to collect

The feature is designed to reduce prescription status enquiries to pharmacies, helping to free up staff time while giving patients clearer updates through the NHS App.

The notification feature runs automatically in the background as pharmacies manage their normal dispensing operations.

A simple but important step in connecting pharmacy workflows with national NHS digital infrastructure.
