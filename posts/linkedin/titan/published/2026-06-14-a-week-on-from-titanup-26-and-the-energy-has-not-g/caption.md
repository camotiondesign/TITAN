A week on from TitanUp 26, and the energy has not gone anywhere.

Usually, a week is long enough for an event to go quiet.

This one hasn’t.

People are still talking about it. Not just the venue, the talks, or the day itself.

The shift underneath it.

Around 350 pharmacy owners came to Birmingham with different businesses, different setups, and one question sitting under it all:

How do you grow when the dispensing model keeps asking for more and handing back less?

What came off the stage was not “work harder”.

It was clearer than that.

✅ Move from items to services, on purpose.
✅ Let the system carry more of the checking, not the team.
✅ Protect the margin the NHS contract keeps eroding.
✅ Work differently, not just faster.

That was the thread running through the day.

Less chasing volume.

More building pharmacy businesses that can hold onto their margin, their time, and their people.

That is what our speakers spent the day making the case for.
