If you work in community pharmacy, there are two people worth getting to know.

Both are independent pharmacy contractors.

Both are board members at @IPCN - Independent Pharmacy Contractors Network.

The only co-operative member-owned pharmacy contractor organisation in the sector.

A group that believes in collaboration over competition.

Owned by contractors. Run by contractors. For contractors.

An ethos of collective support to move the sector forward. Working together, sharing insights, supporting one another.

What that looks like in practice:

Specialised peer-support groups across:

✅ Pharmacy acquisition
✅ NHS contract support
✅ Staff management
✅ IT and PMRs
✅ Robots and automation
✅ Clinical support
✅ Locum shifts
✅ Foundation year

Members sharing what's worked and what hasn't, openly with each other.

They also lobby CPE, DHSC and national bodies, and run grassroots campaigns on the structural challenges facing independents.
