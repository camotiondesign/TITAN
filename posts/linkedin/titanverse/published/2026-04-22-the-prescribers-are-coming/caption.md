The prescribers are coming.

The infrastructure isn't.

September 2026 is five months away.

Every newly qualified pharmacist joins the register as an independent prescriber.

The NHS has already proved the model works.

33,000+ consultations across 164 pathfinder sites.
59% led to prescribing interventions that would have gone to a GP or hospital.
97% of episodes closed without onward referral.

And yet 93% of IPs in community pharmacy still can't use their qualification.

Even inside the funded NHS pilot, with dedicated IT support and ring-fenced prescribing sessions, pharmacists were dealing with multiple platforms that didn't talk to each other, duplicate data entry, and clinical governance held together by workarounds.

If it was that hard in the pilot, imagine the rest of community pharmacy.
