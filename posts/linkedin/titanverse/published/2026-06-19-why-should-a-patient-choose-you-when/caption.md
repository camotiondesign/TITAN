Why should a patient choose you when…

“Pharmacy is becoming Nando’s. Everyone has the same menu.”

That was @Rahul Puri’s point at TitanUp 26.

Weight management, travel, blood pressure, vaccinations, prescribing. 

More pharmacies now offer versions of the same list. The services still matter, but they are no longer enough to set you apart.

What sets you apart is what happens after the patient chooses you.

Two pharmacies can offer the same service and build completely different businesses around it. One treats the appointment as a transaction. The other turns it into a relationship.

That relationship is hard to build when the journey is held together manually.

✅ Bookings need to be simple to manage.
✅ Consultations need to be structured.
✅ Notes need to land in the right place.
✅ Follow-ups cannot rely on memory.
✅ Service history needs to be easy to see.
✅ The next step needs to be clear for the patient and the team.
✅ The whole journey needs to connect smoothly back into the PMR.

That is exactly what Titanverse is built for.

Not another item on the menu, but the system built to help pharmacies run the journey around it.

Or, as Rahul put it:

“The service is not your advantage anymore. The journey is.”
