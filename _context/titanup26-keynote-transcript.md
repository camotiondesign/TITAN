## Tariq Muhammad — CEO keynote (07:03)

Opens with the "look around — competitors or comrades?" frame. Then the P-word setup:

- *"Seven years ago, no one had heard of Titan, and here we are a grassroots movement representing one and a half thousand independent community pharmacists disrupting the sector."*
- *"You refused to accept that it was okay to spend your lives in the back of a pharmacy checking baskets. You refused to accept that it was okay to waste your qualification on sticking labels on boxes."*
- *"Titan didn't make you work better. You took Titan and made it work."*

**The P-word reveal (~10:00):**
- *"The P word, of course, is profit. Wanting profit doesn't make you greedy. Wanting profit does not make you selfish. Wanting profit is simply admitting the reality of being in business."*
- *"It's not pharmacy first, it's finance first. Profit isn't the opposite of care. Profit is what makes care possible."*

**The Pharmacy Plus story (~12:00–14:29):**
- 20 years building the business, quarter of a million items a month, £30m turnover
- October 2012 drug tariff change wiped £1 million off the bottom line
- 12 months later administration, 400 souls lost jobs
- Rebuild → Invatech Health → Titan → the lesson is profit

**Three reasons profit matters:**
1. *"Profit is your lifeline. It's like oxygen, without it you die."*
2. Enables everything else — services, staff, hires, community
3. Valuation and exit — *"if your profit is zero, it doesn't matter what you multiply that number by, your value is zero."*

**Three habits to break:**
1. Dependency on the NHS — *"when 95% of your income is coming from a single source that can change its terms overnight, that is not security, that is vulnerability dressed up as stability."*
2. Giving stuff away for free — MDS, deliveries, advice
3. Guesswork — *"instinct built without data is just hope, and hope is not a business strategy."*

**Three habits to make:**
1. Circle of control — focus energy on what you can change
2. Optimise income and operations (NHS services, dispensing, Titan tools)
3. Build new income streams — *"stack it high, don't sell it cheap. Travel clinics, vaccinations, weight loss, hair loss, TRT, HRT."*

**The five new tools tease:**
1. Titan AI (Level 5 coming)
2. Titanverse as the most comprehensive clinical platform
3. New central controls
4. Guesswork-elimination (predicted income, gross profit visibility)
5. Business-from-your-pocket

**The close (~44:00):**
- *"Titan is not a software company. We are a movement. A movement made up of people like you people who refused to accept what they were told was normal."*
- *"You did not just buy a system, you joined something."*
- *"From today, Titan no longer means PMR. From today, Titan means business."*

## Wahid Muhammad — CTO keynote (47:15) — Level 5 AI section

**Opens on AI (~47:30):**
- *"AI is something that we obviously flaunt, we use it all the time. But it's being probably the most overused two letters on the planet right now."*

**The confession (~48:00):**
- *"We don't actually use AI. I know, I know, that's a shock. I know some of you are going to start leaving, but just wait."*
- *"We don't use AI at all. We build AI. And there is a big, big, big difference of using AI and building AI."*
- *"Many people are using AI. That's going over to ChatGPT or Claude, but that's not good enough when it comes to health tech. It's not good enough when it comes to clinical systems."*

**The team reveal (~50:00):**
- *"We need to rebuild our team and get some of the best talent to join our team."*
- AI Engineering team led by **Professor Mohammed Bilal** (Professor of AI, University of Birmingham) + two PhD-qualified professionals + Mahmood Alam + Taofeek
- Clinical team: Nour Karkach, Jon Flitcroft, Tony Donnelly — *"pharmacists and clinicians having to stroll through tens of thousands of rows of data"*
- Legal & Governance: Monissa Muhammad (in-house solicitor) — *"focused on ensuring that we stay within the legal frameworks, but also allowing us to push the boundaries of what we can actually do with AI"*
- Jeff — aligning to MHRA medical device markers
- External auditing bodies underneath

**The Level 5 launch (~54:00):**
- *"The launch of Level Five Titan AI."*
- *"Level five is absolutely amazing. It is shockingly good. It is a complete new ground up development."*
- *"We're moving away from it being an AI model to it becoming an agentic AI model."*
- *"An agentic AI model basically means there are many AI models that are running in sequence, specialised models that do a certain thing, a certain function. Each and every one of these layers are models that we have built, that we have trained, that we own."*
- *"This isn't ChatGPT, this isn't Claude, or something like that. These are models that we are running on our own infrastructure, in our own data centers."*

**How it works (~55:30):**
- *"Every single prescription that goes through level five is forensically analyzed, pulled apart. It is checked up against the BNF. Not only is it checked against the BNF, it's checked against real live usage of community pharmacy data."*
- *"It's advanced enough to even look at the additional information that the doctor writes, pulling it apart and attaching it back to rebuild a new dosage, ready for the patient to consume."*

**The stat (~57:00):**
- *"We're seeing upwards of 70% full automation at the moment on level five, which is amazing."*
- *"Within the next six to eight months, we predict around 90% plus full automation."*
- *"What does that mean? 90% of your prescriptions won't even go to the pharmacist."*
- *"Within the next couple of years, that number will go bigger and bigger."*

Introduced by Sajid as new "CEO" (running gag). Handles the Titanverse commercial reveal.

- *"You've seen all this amazing technology, and you're really tempted. But at the back of my mind I'm thinking, how much is it going to cost me?"*

**The PSMC framing (~1:28):**

1. Platform (compliance, services, records, mobile)

- Bundles have no expiry, spread across branches

**The commitment offer (~1:39):**

- Includes platform + NHS services app + Titan Business + DemDx + PharmApi integrations + free NHS consultations

## Q&A panel (from ~1:47:45, hosted by Zad Bhatti)

Notable answers:

**Tariq on NHS reliance:**
- *"These habits have been ingrained into us since time began. We can't just ditch the NHS, we can't just flick a switch and move over. This has to be a migration."*

**Hooman on undercutting competitors on private services:**
- *"If you cannot provide a service for a profit, then why are you doing it? You're better off not doing that service. You're devaluing yourself, what you're worth. People will come. If you build the service, they will come, and if you provide a good service at that, they will be willing to pay for that."*

**Wahid on IP prescribing timeline:**
- *"We are firmly committed to making Titanverse your NHS services platform for absolutely everything they're providing. We are already speaking to the NHS about independent prescribing. Our approach isn't just to make it a bolt on."*

**Jeff on operational metrics:**
- *"Traditionally metrics used in pharmacy have been items. So how many items have I dispensed? That's my indicator of success. That's a historical measure. With Titan, because we capture the workflow from arrival to handouts, I would look at the throughput, the workflow in each stage. Where are your bottlenecks? Which staff are performing well?"*

**Tariq on competitors (Apotec, PharmacyX):**
- *"When has technology ever been so sexy in pharmacy, right? This is all as a result of our creation. We started this seven years ago, and now the market has just absolutely snowballed. We take huge pride in that."*

**Wahid on Level 5 AI training requirement:**
- *"Unlike the other levels where it's actually asked you guys to train it effectively, that is not the case. The clinical team's job is to train the AI model. They have been doing for the last year, hundreds and hundreds of thousands of rows of data have been already been pumped into it. It's gone through iteration after iteration of training. The model's ready to go. You switch it on, you'll start hitting those numbers."*

**Tariq on the future / robots:**
- *"My vision for pharmacy, for the future, is we're not limited just to dispensing, we're not limited just to doing these services, not even limited to doing private services. Why can't we take on the health care system? Why can't we take on medical services? Why can't we dream bigger?"*

**Wahid on timeline:**
- Titanverse private services: available now
- Business tools + NHS integrations: coming in the next couple of months
- NHS side timing: dependent on NHS process ("we are going at lightning speed, absolute lightning speed")
