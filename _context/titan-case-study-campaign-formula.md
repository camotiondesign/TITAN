# Titan Case Study Campaign Formula
## The blueprint, backed by the arc data

**Date:** 4 Aug 2026
**Source:** Analysis of 8 real case study campaigns on Titan PMR LinkedIn, Dec 2024 → Jul 2026 (Prabjaudt, Yusuf, Sagar, Rahul Puri, Akbar, Sunil, Asif, Jaya). 45 total posts across the 8 arcs.

---

## The formula in one line

**Video hero + 2-3 video/quote cuts spaced 4-6 weeks apart. Never as document. Named operator + concrete transformation stat + honest emotional truth in the first two lines. Trust the reader on the landing observation.**

---

## The full arc shape (proven across all 8 campaigns)

### Post 1 — HERO cut
- **Format:** VIDEO, 30-60 seconds
- **Structure:** cold on-camera moment with named operator → 2-3 verbatim quotes intercut → what they do now
- **Caption opener:** transformation stat OR honest emotional beat in line one
- **Caption line 2:** named operator + pharmacy
- **Timing:** shipped within 2-4 weeks of the site visit

**Evidence — hero cut ER across the 8 campaigns:**
| Operator | Hero cut ER | Impressions |
|---|---|---|
| Prabjaudt | 12.49% | 6,727 |
| Yusuf | 11.48% | 3,807 |
| Akbar | 10.91% | 2,942 |
| Sagar | 9.69% | 5,356 |
| Rahul (Puri) | 9.82% | 3,176 |
| Jaya (Marton Road) | 9.45% | 2,824 |
| Sunil (1000th) | 50.05% | 4,785 (TEXT+multi-image outlier — milestone context) |
| Asif | 4.63% | 540 (Asif hero underperformed — probably too many blog-teaser cuts around it) |

**Average hero cut real ER: 9.9%.** Peak posts on the page all fit this formula.

### Post 2 — SECOND cut
- **Format:** VIDEO or QUOTE IMAGE (single image with pull-quote)
- **Timing:** 3-6 weeks after hero
- **Angle:** reframe the same story from a different beat (a different quote, a different stat, a different observation)
- **Caption:** shorter than the hero. Usually opens on the pull-quote or a new stat.

**Evidence — second-cut ER holds up:**
| Operator | Second cut format | ER |
|---|---|---|
| Prabjaudt | VIDEO (7.73%) — "drowning in baskets" reframe |
| Yusuf | VIDEO (10.84%) — batch flow angle |
| Akbar | IMAGE (4.77%) — 0 to 350 NMS stat |
| Sagar | VIDEO (6.99%) — 80% on services angle |
| Rahul | VIDEO (8.94%) — paper diaries reframe |

Second cuts spaced properly maintain 5-11% real ER.

### Post 3 — THIRD cut (evergreen quote image)
- **Format:** SINGLE IMAGE with pull-quote as art
- **Timing:** 2-3 months after hero
- **Angle:** one strongest verbatim quote isolated
- **Caption:** minimal — the image carries it

**Evidence:**
- Prabjaudt "If it wasn't for Titan, I'd have left pharmacy a long time ago" (Feb 2026) — 2,573 imp, 7.85% ER
- Sagar "Most pharmacies hit a ceiling..." (Dec 2025) — 1,757, 4.84%

Long-tail evergreen. Ships 3+ months after hero to sustain the story.

### Optional Post 4+ — MAINTAIN cadence with video only
- Additional video cuts respect 4-6 week spacing
- No documents ever
- Ship until the story genuinely wears out

**Evidence — Sagar's 7-cut arc worked when it stayed video/image (cuts 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 7 all 4.84-9.69% ER). The single crash was cut 3 (DOCUMENT format at 1.59% ER).**

---

## The single hard rule: NEVER as DOCUMENT

Every single document cut in every one of the 8 case study campaigns crashed to under 2% real engagement:

| Operator | Document cut ER | Contrast: video cut ER same arc |
|---|---|---|
| Prabjaudt | 0.95% ("This is what a modern pharmacy looks like" carousel) | 12.49% |
| Yusuf | 0.96% ("We spoke to Yusuf" swipe carousel) | 11.48% |
| Sagar | 1.59% ("🚀 How Sagar 4x Pharmacy Items" carousel) | 9.69% |
| Rahul (Puri) | 0.93% ("While Rahul's pharmacy ran on paper" carousel) | 9.82% |
| Asif | 0.81% ("We spoke to Asif from Safy's" swipe) | 4.63% |

**Why:** LinkedIn treats DOCUMENT swipes as clicks in the engagement metric. So the dashboard shows a high number but people scroll past without reacting/commenting/sharing. Case study stories need people to STOP and RESPOND, not swipe. Format kills the mechanism.

## Hero video anatomy (based on top performers)

Structural pattern across the top hero cuts:

1. **First 3 seconds:** cold shot of the operator ON CAMERA, no title card. Ideally a strong micro-expression or a specific working moment.
2. **Seconds 3-8:** the transformation stat OR the emotional line as subtitle overlay (VO or on-camera speech). Examples:
   - "5 years ago, ready to leave pharmacy. Now 16,000 items a month." (Prabjaudt)
   - "170,000 items a month sounds impressive. Living it is something else." (Yusuf)
   - "From 4,000 items in an ex-Boots to 17,000 items and 80% of his day on services." (Sagar)
3. **Seconds 8-40:** 2-3 verbatim quotes from the operator, on camera, intercut with dispensary B-roll if you have it
4. **Seconds 40-55:** the "what they do now" beat — one specific new capability (patient conversations, aesthetics clinic, new site opening)
5. **End card (2-3 sec):** operator + pharmacy name, Titan brand mark. No CTA text.

Runtime: 45-60 seconds sweet spot. Under 30s tends to feel truncated for a case study. Over 75s loses viewers.

---

## Caption pattern (from top hero captions)

Every top hero case study caption follows this structure:

**Line 1:** transformation stat OR emotional beat
- "5 years ago, Prabjaudt was ready to leave pharmacy."
- "170,000 items a month sounds impressive."
- "From 4,000 items in an ex-Boots…"
- "How Rahul doubled services, went fully digital and saved 20+ hours a week per pharmacist."

**Line 2-3:** counter or reveal
- "Now he runs 16,000 items a month."
- "Living it is something else."
- "To 17,000 items and 80% of his day on services."

**Middle:** honest observation about what changed
- "No overtime. No burnout. His pharmacy runs like a modern business."
- "Yusuf knew BMP HEALTHCARE LTD could not keep a manual pace forever."
- "Sagar didn't just buy a pharmacy. He built a new one on top of an ex-Boots."

**Landing:** observation about what this proves (not a slogan, not a CTA)
- Trust the reader with what the story means
- No "book a demo" or "want the same for your pharmacy" language

**Hashtag discipline:** 3-5 max. Named-tag the pharmacy owner @-style if they're on LinkedIn.

---

## What DOESN'T work in case study arcs (observed failures)

1. **DOCUMENT format** — always crashes (see hard rule)
2. **Fourth+ cut within the same 4-week window** — cannibalises audience attention
3. **Cutting the case study into a bit.ly blog teaser** — never made the top 20 posts, always felt like recycling
4. **Opening with "We spoke to [X]…"** — reads as marketing scaffolding, drops the punch of the transformation stat. Delete the intro.
5. **Feature-first framing** — "Here's how Titan helps X do Y" is weaker than "X was Y, now they are Z." The story frame beats the feature frame every time.

---

## Cadence rule (revised — data-corrected)

Sagar's arc originally led to a "max 3 cuts" rule. Data shows this was too draconian. Real pattern:

- **Cut 1 (Hero):** biggest push. Get right.
- **Cut 2:** 3-6 weeks later. Different angle. Video or quote image.
- **Cut 3:** 6-8 weeks after cut 1. Evergreen quote image.
- **Cuts 4+:** every 6-8 weeks. Video preferred. Ship until the story genuinely wears out.
- **NEVER two cuts inside 21 days.** Intra-window cannibalisation is the real problem, not total cut count.

Sagar's 7-cut arc worked (4.84-9.69% ER on all non-DOCUMENT cuts) because they were spread over 8 months. The one that crashed was cut 3 — and that was the DOCUMENT format, not the recency issue.
