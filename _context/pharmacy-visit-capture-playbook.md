# Pharmacy Visit Capture Playbook

Repeatable capture framework for any pharmacy visit — Cam alone, or Cam with a Titan team member (Sajid, Hooman, Wahid, Zad, etc.). Every visit should produce a defined content package covering five distinct roles, so a single shoot day yields 3–5 posts spread over the following 2–3 weeks instead of one over-worked recap.

Built off the "content package per filming day" frame from the 14 July Titan strategy pass.

---

## The five content roles per visit

Every visit's raw material should be captured with one of these five roles in mind. If a role is missing, the shoot didn't do its job.

| # | Role | Question it answers | Typical asset |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Hero** | What's the flagship story of this visit? | Short film 60–90s OR multi-image essay |
| 2 | **Proof** | Why does this pharmacy work? | 60–90s owner testimonial OR one workflow moment |
| 3 | **Insight** | What did they figure out that others should copy? | Owner talking to camera about the shift |
| 4 | **Ecosystem** | How does the tech/partners/team connect? | Shots of hardware + software + team in flow |
| 5 | **Social texture** | What did the day feel like? | Stories / Reels / candid photos |

If you come away with only social texture, the visit was a hangout. If you come away with only proof and insight, the visit will feel corporate. Aim for all five.

## Standing shot list — capture every visit

### Photos (multi-image post fuel)

- **Exterior** — pharmacy front with signage, at least one straight-on, one from an angle
- **Arrival** — team greeting at the door, handshakes, "the walk in"
- **Wide dispensary** — the room as it works, real people at real stations
- **Screen closeups** — Titan running on their monitor(s), timestamps visible, real workflow open
- **Hero portrait** — owner standing in their space, arms crossed or hands on bench, warm expression
- **Team candid** — 2–4 team members mid-task, natural (not lined-up-for-photo)
- **Details** — a specific piece of hardware (robot, board, MDS trays), a busy checking column, coffee on the counter, paperwork stacked
- **Goodbye** — the shot at the door on the way out, group or handshake

**Rule:** at least one photo from each of these 8 categories. Any more is bonus.

### Video

- **Owner talking to camera (60–90s)** — landscape 16:9, mic'd, seated or standing in their pharmacy. Two or three questions asked off-camera, cut so the owner carries the answer.
- **Workflow moment (30–45s)** — a real thing happening. A basket moving through the board, someone using Titan, a robot picking. No narration needed.
- **Cam-with-Titan-team conversation (documentary-style)** — if a Titan person is with you, capture them and the owner mid-conversation. Mic both sides. Stand back. Let it be natural.
- **Titan person walk-and-talk (60s)** — the team member's take on what they saw, filmed after leaving the pharmacy. Car park works. Their fresh reaction.
- **B-roll** — 3–5 seconds each of anything visually interesting. Wide of the pharmacy from across the road, close-ups of medicine shelves, screen glow at night, the team's badges/lanyards.

### Audio requirements

- **Two-mic minimum** when you're capturing team conversations (one on each speaker)
- **Boom or lav on the owner** for the talking-to-camera moment — don't rely on camera mic
- **Record ambient room tone** for 30 seconds somewhere quiet — always useful in the edit
- **Cam's own thoughts, voice memo, on the drive home** — the "what stood out today" reflection, for future context

## Standing question set — for owner interview

Ask these, in this order. Skip any that don't fit.

1. **"Walk me through a typical day here — what does the team actually do?"** (opens on their pride, not their pitch)
2. **"What changed most when you switched to Titan?"** (the story hook)
3. **"What surprised you — good or bad — about the change?"** (unexpected beats, authentic)
4. **"What would you say to another pharmacy owner thinking about it?"** (the shareable soundbite)
5. **"What's next for you here — private services, another site, something else?"** (forward-look)

Ask them off-camera. Cut so the owner carries the answer.

## Post-visit deliverables map — the 2–3 week rollout

Each visit produces roughly this cadence of posts:

### Day-of (same evening)

- **Stories dump** on Instagram + Facebook (candid social texture — arrivals, dispensary reveal, handshakes)
- Optional single photo on the LI page if you have a great "we visited X today" shot — but only if it earns its slot, otherwise wait

### Within 7 days

- **Multi-image post (LI + FB + IG)** — the arrival/team/handshake photos as the "here's the pharmacy we visited" hero. 4–8 images. Caption uses the ChatGPT question-hook / editorial style.
- **Short vertical clip (30–45s, TT + IG + YT Shorts)** — the strongest owner soundbite pulled from their to-camera interview

### Week 2 (7–14 days after)

- **60–90s LinkedIn video** — the polished visit story. Weave 2–3 moments: hero portrait + workflow moment + owner insight. Follows the same three-segment structure as the Sajid Innovation Day mixed edit. Cross-post to FB.
- OR single-image quote card featuring the strongest owner line — designed in Titan brand system

### Week 3 (14–21 days after) — optional

- **Longform on YouTube** — full owner interview if the material earned it (5–15 min). Push to LI with a short single-image or teaser clip.
- OR feature spotlight — one specific Titan feature the pharmacy mentioned, built into a carousel or single-image post

That's **3–5 posts per visit**, spread so the visit story keeps landing without a burst-and-fade pattern.

## Special case — visits with a Titan team member (Sajid, Hooman, etc.)

Additional captures beyond the standing list:

- **Titan person + owner mid-conversation** — this is your documentary layer. Mic both, stand back
- **Titan person walk-and-talk** — their reflection immediately after (car park, on the drive)
- **The handshake/hug at arrival AND departure** — gives you a proper narrative bracket
- **Group shot at end** — team + Titan person + you if it fits

The mixed-edit format works especially well here — you can weave the owner's testimony with the Titan person's reflection like the Sajid Innovation Day cut did.

---

## Roadtrip / multi-pharmacy visits — additional captures

When visiting 2+ pharmacies in a day:

- **Between-pharmacy driving footage** — Sajid/Hooman/etc. discussing what they just saw, reflecting on what's coming
- **Arrival at each pharmacy** — the door handshake shot at every stop
- **Consistent framing** — same lens, same crop for the exterior and owner-portrait shots so a multi-pharmacy carousel reads as a set
- **One connecting question** — ask every pharmacist the same one thing, so you can cut a "5 owners answer: [X]" piece later

For the Sajid + Hooman road trip Thu 23 Jul, this format has a natural narrative: **"The road to Innovation Day"** — three pharmacies + the arrival at Marton Road as the final beat.

## Consent + fact-check flags

Every visit:

- **Verbal consent on camera** — pharmacy team OK with being filmed, ideally recorded on camera
- **Confirm spelling of names + titles** — written down before you leave, for lower-thirds
- **Ask which platforms they'd prefer you tag** — LI handle, IG handle, whether they have TikTok
- **Product claims from the owner** — attribute to them, don't restate as brand claim
- **Comparative claims about other PMRs** — keep as their opinion, brand voice stays neutral

## What the Notion post should look like when you slot a visit

Suggested naming: `TitanPMR_[Pharmacy]_[Angle]_[Format]`

Example: `TitanPMR_Whitehill_RemoteTraining_SingleImage` (matches the Whitehill post 14 Jul).

Fields to fill:
- **Campaign** → the umbrella (TitanUp 2026 / Innovation Day / Road Trip / Standalone)
- **Strategic Role** → Destination / Dispensary / Clinical Platform / Shopfront based on what the pharmacy is showing
- **Content Shelf Life** → typically Timely (has to ship within 2–3 weeks of visit)
- **Editorial Priority** → P1 for the hero, P2 for the amplification pieces
- **Idea field** → notes on which of the 5 roles this post serves + source file paths

---

## The larger point

A visit isn't one post. It's a package. When you plan the shoot, plan the five roles. When you edit, cut for those roles. When you publish, spread the pieces across 2–3 weeks so each one lands with room to breathe.

Skip a role because the visit didn't give you the material — that's fine. But don't try to squeeze five roles out of two clips. Better to visit again.
