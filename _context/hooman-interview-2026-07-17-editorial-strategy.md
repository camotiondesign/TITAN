### Category: Automation Technique

#### 6. "Clean vs Dirty lane: the technique your dispensary might not be using"
- **Core takeaway:** Splitting workflow by validation type. Clean lane (full-pack, non-CD, non-cytotoxic) needs only barcode validation. Dirty/needs-check lane needs physical check. Different skill mixes on each. Works at branch or hub level.
- **Why now:** Never covered publicly like this before. Unique to Titan.
- **Audience:** High-volume pharmacies, hub operators, technicians, curious owners
- **Format:** Educational carousel (6-7 slides) with diagrams
- **Platforms:** LI, FB
- **Engagement:** HIGH — genuinely new insight
- **Evergreen**
- **Timestamps:** 20:26 – 23:20

#### 7. "Barcode validation at labelling — not picking. Why the difference matters."
- **Core takeaway:** Titan validates at the labelling step. Won't generate a label until you've scanned and validated. Other PMRs validate at picking only — wrong label can still land on right pack.
- **Why now:** Competitors are adding barcode validation now but doing it at the wrong step.
- **Audience:** Owners doing PMR due diligence
- **Format:** Explainer video (Hooman, 60-90 sec) OR carousel with diagrams
- **Platforms:** LI, FB
- **Engagement:** MEDIUM — nuanced but sharp
- **Evergreen**
- **Timestamps:** 41:47 – 42:41
