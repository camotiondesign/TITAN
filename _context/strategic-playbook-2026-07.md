# Titan Strategic Playbook: July 2026

A full-operation review of Titan PMR and Titanverse content produced by CMotionDesign between Dec 2024 and 10 Jul 2026, framed as a Head of Marketing / Creative Strategy Director would assess it: what has worked, what has not, what patterns are showing up in the data, where the assumptions in the current strategy files hold, where they do not, and where the next quarter's investment should go.

Data source anchor: 421 LinkedIn posts with organic metrics across both brands (`analytics/aggregated-linkedin-metrics.json`, refreshed 13 Jul 2026); the pre-computed cohort analysis in `exports/content-intelligence.json` (409 posts scored); the two brand indexes; the merged voice canon (`_context/titan-voice-canon.md`); the audience-voice signal from titanup.club polls; the TitanUp 26 keynote transcript; and the interview and case-study library. Every claim below is traceable. Where a claim cannot be, it is flagged.

Total organic footprint measured: **540,205 impressions, 135,704 engagements, 421 posts**. Titan PMR contributes roughly 89 percent of the impressions and 82 percent of the posts; Titanverse the rest.

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Titan's content operation is in a stronger craft position than it has ever been and a weaker distribution position than it was nine months ago. Both statements need to sit next to each other.

The craft position is strong. Quality Engagement Score (QES) has almost doubled in the last three months, from a 27.9 rolling average to 53.2, a 90.7 percent lift (`content-intelligence.json` trend block). The recent voice canon reads like a house voice, not a set of clever tricks: Jaya's "the tools Titan has built support the whole pharmacy ecosystem", Sajid's "you didn't get into pharmacy to miss your life", Geoff's "40,000 items a month, no chaos, no overload", the Ramadan post's "no water, no food". Named operator plus specific number plus honest human beat is now the reliable spine. TitanUp 26 delivered a genuinely repositioned company narrative, from "PMR" to "business", from "AI-powered" as a feature to "we build AI" as an identity, and from Titanverse as a services layer to Titanverse as a business platform.

The distribution position is weaker. Average impressions per post fell from 2,758 in Nov 2025 to 900 in May 2026 and 497 in the first ten days of July 2026 (`aggregated-linkedin-metrics.json` monthly rollup). Post volume rose from a Titan PMR baseline of 17 posts a month to 30 in May and 27 in June, but each post is reaching fewer people. The lift in QES is real, but it is happening across a shrinking audience per post. The engine got tighter and the reach got thinner at the same time.

Six patterns emerge from the data. First, event content is the single most reliable high-performance lane: six of the top ten QES posts of the entire dataset are Pharmacy Show or TitanUp coverage. Second, named operator plus specific scale number plus honesty beat is the dominant winning shape (Prab 12 comments, Jaya 10, Sajid 8, Geoff Malpas repeatedly, Khal Lansdales 2,778 engagements). Third, policy-to-practice-with-a-stance content drives reposts more reliably than any other type (Stop Nomination Ping-Pong 14 reposts, GTIN 12, Prescribers Are Coming 5 on the newer brand). Fourth, boosting cold impressions destroys engagement: boosted posts averaged 0.1 percent to 1.1 percent engagement rate against a 25.25 percent organic average on comparable content. Fifth, formula compliance is genuinely poor: 51 back-to-back post-type violations, 61 customer rotation violations, 12 tier-one deficit months, and a headline compliance score of 2 out of what looks like a 100-scale system. Sixth, the "media company that happens to sell" test, strip the product mention, does the post still have value, is failing on around 25 percent of the library, particularly on generic ecosystem posts and short customer clips.

The single strongest pattern worth doubling down on is not memes and not carousels. It is the multi-image post shot on a real visit day. Multi-image posts average 44.8 QES against a 30 overall average and drive both reach (1,818 avg impressions) and reposts. The Jaya, Prab, Marton Road, Lansdales, Balance Street and Head Office Day posts all live in this shape. This is the format that most consistently answers the audience-voice signal ("show me exactly how it works", 47 percent of TitanUp poll voters) and it is the format the pharmacy-visit capture playbook is built to produce.

The single biggest gap Cam should close is owned-audience capture. Every current metric is measuring rung five (LinkedIn followers) of Pulizzi's Subscription Hierarchy, which is the second-weakest rung there is. There is no live email programme, the case study pages have no PDF gates, and titanup.club growth is not being deliberately routed from LinkedIn. Every visit shoot right now is producing rung-five content and terminating rung-five. This is the single move on the board that changes the game, not the caption craft that is now working.

The most surprising result: `customer_transformation` posts, which the content-formula names as the Tier-1 anchor, actually average 19.8 QES, the worst of any classified type after engagement questions (11.1) and meme/relatable (21.9). That is not because customer transformation stories do not work; the eight-post Sagar cohort averages 19, the Rahul cohort averages 24.3, the Yusuf cohort averages 27.7. It is because too many of them repeat the same customer, drop specificity, or ship as short clips rather than the multi-image or carousel shape that actually carries them. This finding is a compliance failure hiding as a format failure. It is the most correctable item on the entire board.

Recommendation ranking, forward focus, and kill list are in Sections 13 to 15.

## 2. Where Titan content is today (state of play)

**Volumes.** Titan PMR has published roughly 359 pieces of measurable LinkedIn content in the review window, Titanverse roughly 79. Combined that is 421 posts with organic metrics across 19 months. Baseline post cadence on Titan PMR was 17 posts a month across Feb-Sep 2025. That climbed to 27-30 posts a month across May-Jun 2026. The July 2026 partial month is running below trend.

**Impressions.** Titan PMR has generated approximately 595,000 organic impressions, Titanverse 66,000. The combined total (540,205 in the 421-post organic set) reflects that boosted posts are excluded from the aggregate; the master index total is slightly higher because it treats boost separately. Median reach per post on Titan PMR sits around 900-1,400 depending on month. Median reach on Titanverse sits around 500-700, with peaks in the low thousands.

**Engagement patterns.** Titan PMR's average engagement rate reads as 22.4 percent in the master index, but this is inflated by the carousel-swipe artefact LinkedIn treats as a click. The Quality Engagement Score developed for the analysis (`analytics/linkedin-performance-analysis.md`) strips that noise. Under QES, the overall dataset average is 30 (per-thousand-impressions, weighted for comments and reposts).

**Trend direction.** The three-month rolling QES has jumped 90.7 percent recently (recent 53.2 vs prior 27.9). Recent monthly averages: 2026-05 at 38.3, 2026-06 at 36.1, 2026-07 at 85.3 (small sample). Impressions have moved the other way, down 17.4 percent over the same window. Recent monthly averages: 2026-05 at 908, 2026-06 at 1,132, 2026-07 at 515.

**Best-performing type by average QES:** cultural_calendar (45.9 avg, only 3 percent of recent content), event_content (35.0 avg, 9 percent of recent content), and thought_leadership (33.5 avg). Cultural moments, Eid, Ramadan, Valentine's, punch far above their share of the calendar. Customer transformation, the content-formula's flagship anchor, only averages 19.8. That gap is one of the most important findings in this document.

**Best-performing format by average QES:** short_video (46.9, small sample), multi-image (44.8), video (30.8), single-image (30.3), carousel (21.4). Multi-image is now the highest-yield format that produces at volume. Carousels remain necessary for stance and framework work but are no longer the reach lever they were in Apr 2025.

**Best day to post (Titan PMR organic):** Wednesday. n=80, average 1,447 impressions, 146 comments, 231 reposts. Tuesday and Thursday follow closely. Friday underperforms on reach (1,202) but still generates comments. Monday under-samples (n=42) suggesting deliberate under-publishing.

## 3. SWOT: Titan PMR

**Strengths**

The named-operator library is the single strongest asset in the operation. Sagar (Brighton Hill, 4x growth to 17,000 items), Prab (Priory, 16,000 items, "ready to leave pharmacy"), Rahul Puri (Puri, 20 hours saved a week), Yusuf (BMP, 170,000 items), Jaya (Marton Road, all-in on the stack), Geoff (Malpas, 40,000 items with calm), Khal (Lansdales, four sites), Akbar (Drayton Prime), Sunil (Hollywood, 15,000 items), Sajid (personal-life-vs-scale angle), Tariq (P-word), Wahid (Level 5 AI), Hooman (£25 offer). No competitor has anything comparable. Cegedim, Positive Solutions and RxWeb between them do not produce a single named-operator story per quarter (`analytics/competitor-social-audit.md`).

The voice is now recognisable. The canon fingerprint holds: verbatim beats paraphrase, honesty beat over polished claim, fragments carrying meaning, numbers as characters, product entering late, British and dry, closes trusting the reader (`titan-voice-canon.md` Part 3). Sajid's "You didn't get into pharmacy to miss your life", Ramadan's "No water. No food. And they will still be on their feet. Still dispensing. Still checking. Still advising patients", Geoff's "No chaos. No overload. No weekends lost to the dispensary." That is not template writing.

Event content is a genuinely repeatable win: Pharmacy Show 2025 produced six of the ten top QES posts of the review window, TitanUp 26 produced 64 pieces of content across the campaign arc with 55,287 impressions and 286 reposts. The playbook for a two-week event cluster is proven.

The founder voices are differentiated and usable. Tariq direct (P-word, three habits, six analogies), Wahid direct ("we don't use AI, we build AI"), Hooman direct ("if you're thriving, we're thriving"). Retold versions of the same content fail. Direct versions work. That register discipline is now documented (`titan-founder-voices.md`).

**Weaknesses**

Formula compliance is genuinely poor: 51 back-to-back post-type violations, 61 customer rotation violations, and 12 months where the Tier-1 minimum was missed (`content-intelligence.json`). The formula was written to stop the patterns that killed the golden era; the operation has been drifting back into them.

Customer transformation posts (the anchor slot) average 19.8 QES, worst of any classified type after engagement questions. This is not that transformations do not work. It is that they are being shipped as short clips, at rotation frequencies that fatigue the audience, and often without the four-ingredient recipe (named person, specific number, before/after, emotional hook) that the content formula requires.

Reach per post has fallen consistently while volume has risen. From 2,758 average impressions in Nov 2025 to 900 in May 2026. Publishing more and reaching fewer per piece is the classic sign that either the algorithm is throttling volume, the content is drifting from what the audience picked up before, or both. The volume ramp coincided with the Feb 2026 dual-brand cadence increase; correlation does not prove causation, but it is the strongest signal in the monthly rollup.

Vanity metric dependency. LinkedIn engagement rate is inflated by carousel swipes. Every internal reporting document that quotes engagement rate rather than QES is over-reporting.

Founder voice on the brand page, not on Tariq's own page. Tariq direct content works. Tariq retold does not. Yet almost none of Tariq's monthly Pharmacy Business column output shows up on his personal LinkedIn or as anchor content on the Titan PMR page.

**Opportunities**

Multi-image posts are the highest-yield format available and are systematically under-used. Only 15 posts in 421 use the format, but it averages 44.8 QES (against 30 overall) and 1,818 impressions (against 1,197 for single image, 1,169 for video). The pharmacy-visit capture playbook is already designed to produce this format at cadence. Every visit day should now default to multi-image as the hero deliverable.

The email leg does not exist. Pulizzi's rung 1 (email subscribers, the most-owned audience rung) is entirely absent. Every visit shoot, TitanUp registration, case study download, and LinkedIn post is currently accumulating rented audience. Launching even a monthly newsletter (Tariq's column, customer story of the month, framework drop, per the strategy file) would move the game from where it is now.

Tariq's print archive is a mine of unused frameworks and analogies (`titan-content-foundations.md` Section 8). Bricklayer, dentist, Tesla, Amazon, boiling frog, skyscraper: six analogies documented, published, and not once on LinkedIn as a standalone. Each is a carousel or single-image post waiting to be written.

Pharmacy meme territory is empty in the UK category. ClickUp built a $9B valuation partly on B2B meme content. Cegedim, Positive Solutions, RxWeb, Pharmacy2U, Phlo all publish as if the year is 2015. Titan has already tested humour (Pharmacist Battery Meme 5,747 impressions, PharmAppy Meme, DSPT Meme, Pick Your Player 3,892 engagements) and the audience responded. The meme lane has been thin in Mar-Jul 2026 despite being validated by data.

Owned-audience routing. Only three of the current cadence's CTAs point at owned destinations (titanup.club, blog, or email). The rest terminate on LinkedIn. Redirecting Wednesday and Thursday posts to owned destinations is a change of caption line, not a change of workload.

**Threats**

The Titanverse repivot creates a fact-discipline risk. Wahid explicitly separated LIVE, COMING and FUTURE features at TitanUp 26 (`titanup-26-announcements.md`). Any content that describes COMING features as present, or FUTURE features as imminent, is a credibility failure in the highest-visibility lane. This risk grew when the repivot happened; the website has not yet caught up, so content is the primary vehicle for the new positioning.

Community pharmacy is under structural pressure. NHS funding remains constrained. The 2024 Pharmacy First rollout has not delivered the volumes projected. IPCN is still forming as an independent voice. Independent prescriber September 2026 registration change is coming. If Titan's content over-claims into that pressure, audience trust erodes. If it under-engages, the category conversation happens without Titan in it. The middle position (the "policy-to-practice with a stance" cluster) is the only safe lane.

Competitor imitation. Cegedim and PharmOutcomes have both increased LinkedIn output modestly in 2026. If any competitor hires a real content lead, the meme, human story, or founder-voice lane closes. First-mover advantage is real but not permanent.

Reliance on a small number of star customer stories. Sagar, Prab, Rahul, Yusuf and Geoff carry a disproportionate share of the top-performing content. Every one of them is a business partner, not just a customer. If one relationship changes, a chunk of the story library becomes unusable overnight.

## 4. SWOT: Titanverse

**Strengths**

The POV carousel format has a clear ceiling. "POV: You start a consultation" (2025-11-11) delivered 36.3 QES on a small base; "Pharmacy is shifting fast" (2025-12-02) delivered 52.4; "Paper feels impersonal" (2025-12-16) delivered 34.8. That's a repeatable business-model-shift shape that no competitor has claimed.

Yusuf and Rahul Puri anchor the customer voice. Yusuf's product review video (37.9 QES), AI testimonial image (30.8), AI clinical sources short video (41.9), and BMP case study video (28.1) all outperform the page average. Rahul contributes 18 posts across the two brands with a 24.3 QES average, including "It's limitless to what you can do in your pharmacy" (33.8 QES) and "I've been a prescriber for three years and Titanverse..." (25.6).

The Prescribers Are Coming carousel (Apr 2026, 1,207 impressions, 104.14 percent engagement rate) is Titanverse's strongest evidence-led stance shape. The six-word hook, the sourced numbers, and the September 2026 register anchor combine into a template the page has not yet copied.

The TitanUp 26 repivot puts a genuinely bigger story on Titanverse's plate: not just a services layer, but a business and finance platform. Wahid's on-stage repositioning ("your business platform, your finance platform, your business in your pocket") is the biggest positioning shift the brand has had.

**Weaknesses**

The "one platform" positioning is genuinely fatigued. The analysis file lists 12 different captions across a 44-post window that say essentially the same thing (`linkedin-performance-analysis.md` Titanverse section). The audience has heard it. Reach on those posts has dropped from ~1,200 to ~500.

Sample size is still thin. 79 posts across nearly two years is not enough to lock findings with confidence. Every insight on Titanverse should be treated as directional, not final.

Zero-comment posts are common. Half the Titanverse dataset generates no comments (`titan-voice-canon.md` note on the retired comments-weighted filter). The engagement floor is lower than PMR's.

The Titanverse repivot creates a content backlog. Every LIVE, COMING and FUTURE feature (`titanup-26-announcements.md`) needs its own content lane. That is roughly two to three months of production work if approached at pace. Currently the page is treating this as one post at a time, not as a repositioning campaign.

**Opportunities**

Wahid's repivot line ("business platform, finance platform, business in your pocket") is the single most important sentence to lead with in H2 2026. Every Titanverse post between now and September should be either a demonstration of that thesis or a set-up for it.

Named operator stories are underweight on Titanverse. Only Yusuf and Rahul appear consistently. Glen (Knights), Hamal (S&G, three calendars), Dervis (Healthy-U, camera brand riddle), Rahul Puri specifically on Titanverse, Prab on Titanverse features are all under-featured. The customer voice canon lists eight strong Titanverse entries; the actual publishing cadence has produced maybe three of them per quarter.

The IP September 2026 register change is a macro anchor no competitor has claimed. Every month for the next two months has an obvious stance post attached to that date. The Prescribers Are Coming template already exists.

Business-model-shift framing is under-produced. "Two pharmacies, same building, different future", "Pharmacy is shifting fast, money is in services", "From dispensing to health hub", these are among the top performers, and there have been fewer than one a month across the review window.

**Threats**

Titanverse pricing was a headline TitanUp 26 announcement (£25/site/month, unlimited users, backdated for pioneers). The offer window closed 12 Jun 2026. Any content referencing that offer as live is a factual error.

Sponsor and partner content risk. PharmAppy (Cira/Adra), Meditech, DemDx, Allied all made announcements at TitanUp 26 that fall under the sponsor-stat verification rule (`titanup-26-announcements.md`). Repeating those claims without independent verification is a credibility risk that lands on Titanverse first because it's the ecosystem-facing brand.

Confusion with Titan PMR. If Titanverse content reads as PMR content, or vice versa, the layer distinction that the four-layer stack framework depends on is compromised. "Would a reader know what layer they're in?" is the test in the strategy file, and around 15 percent of recent posts fail it.

## 5. PESTLE: the UK pharmacy landscape shaping content

**Political.** The Pharmacy First scheme (launched Jan 2024) is now two years in and its consultation-count projections are widely regarded as under-delivering. Pharmacy contract negotiations remain fraught (the "Pharmacy First Walk Away" post generated 90 percent+ engagement rate in Jun 2025 because the story was real). NHS England's structural reorganisation continues. Government messaging on "clinical services in community pharmacy" is louder than the underlying funding supports. The political story creates a large number of policy-to-practice-with-a-stance content opportunities. Titan has claimed a handful; it should claim more, and Tariq's polarising print stances ("The NHS Pharmacy contract is fundamentally flawed and the government has no money") are calibrated exactly for this environment.

**Economic.** Community pharmacy margin is under intense pressure. The audience-voice poll from titanup.club (`titan-audience-voice.md` Section 2) shows 41 percent of respondents naming stock as their primary margin bleed and 24 percent naming time as their primary services-growth barrier. Mounjaro pricing (Aug 2025 carousel: 3.4K impressions, 104 percent engagement rate) is exemplary of the reactive lane. The £158M clawback (Jan 2026) and concessions volatility are recurring. Titanverse's business-and-finance platform pivot lands on the economic pain the audience has already named. This is the single most under-served content lane relative to audience-voice signal.

**Social.** Two large social currents matter. First, the profession's identity shift from "counting pills" to "clinical services", the Pharmacy Myths carousel (May 2025, 2,118 impressions, 109 percent engagement rate) named it, the "Pick Your Player" carousel gamified it. Second, workforce wellbeing: Sajid HOD Family Life (49 QES), Sajid Go Home On Time (8 comments), Akshay's "What pharmacy owner gets six weeks off" (1,012 impressions, 5 comments). Content that treats the pharmacist as a human, not a resource, resonates disproportionately. Titan owns this lane in the UK by default because no one else is publishing in it.

**Technological.** AI in healthcare is the dominant sector conversation. Wahid's TitanUp 26 stance ("We don't actually use AI. We build AI. And there is a big, big difference.") is the sharpest brand differentiation on offer. The Level 5 launch (agentic AI, 70 percent full automation, prediction of 90 percent+) is the single largest concrete claim any UK pharmacy tech vendor has on the table. Oracle (natural-language business intelligence, first wave 2027) is a bigger claim still. Both need careful content discipline (see fact-check rules in Section 12).

**Legal / regulatory.** The independent prescribing register change (Sep 2026) is now weeks away. Every newly qualified pharmacist will register as an IP. The infrastructure to deploy them at pharmacy scale does not exist yet. The Prescribers Are Coming carousel is one post; this should be a five-post series across Aug-Sep. GTIN barcode safety (7,338 impressions, 12 reposts) is the exemplar of the safety-with-hook lane, and there are three or four equivalent MHRA / GPhC updates a year.

**Environmental.** Not a live content lane for either brand. Delivery-emissions and pharmacy-in-the-net-zero-NHS conversations exist but are not audience-signal-validated. Deprioritise.

## 6. Campaign retrospectives

### 6.1 TitanUp 26 (7 June 2026)

**What shipped.** 64 pieces of content across a February-to-July arc, 55,287 total impressions, 63 comments, 286 reposts. Peak posts: Week On carousel 2,203 impressions, "The moment has arrived" 2,001, "What a day" 1,975, QR Code Teaser 1,910, "Titanup Venue Recce" 1,613.

**What worked.** The QR Code Teaser (Feb 26) is the standout of the campaign build-up: 1,910 impressions, 10 reposts, 6 comments, and the highest new-follower count of the whole review window (4 new followers on a single post). The teaser format proved the mystery-then-reveal pattern from the analysis file. The event-week cluster (Jun 7-8 posts: "Just Getting Started", "The Moment Has Arrived", "What A Day") delivered coordinated reach and generated a satisfying end-of-day narrative. The "Week On" carousel (Jun 14) captured post-event energy at 2,203 impressions and 8 reposts.

**What did not.** The three loop videos in the middle of the campaign (Register Loop, First 100, TitanUp Register Loop) averaged 394 impressions and 5.2 percent engagement rate. The boosted Launch Video (Mar 4) generated 14,311 impressions on paid but 0.1 percent engagement rate against the organic version's 7.6 percent. Boosting a promo video to cold audience burned the impression budget without moving anyone toward the event.

**The keynote is under-exploited.** The 2h13m keynote is a content mine (`titanup26-keynote-transcript.md`). Tariq's P-word doctrine, Three Habits framework, Pharmacy Plus story; Wahid's Level 5 launch, "we don't use AI, we build AI", Titanverse repivot; Hooman's fairness argument, the £25 offer construction; Zad's panel, that is 20 carousel posts, 15 quote cards, 6 hero videos, and a full YouTube programme. The transcript file is dated 14 Jul 2026 (three days ago), suggesting the exploitation phase is only just starting. It should now sprint.

**Lesson.** The Pharmacy Show 2025 template (announcement → build-up → live day one → live day two → recap montage → individual clips) is proven. TitanUp 26 delivered the first half well and is still under-executing the second half. Every future in-person event should have the full six-stage post-event arc scheduled before the event happens.

### 6.2 Lansdales (Khal Khaliq)

The 2026-06-23 carousel ("What's the first thing you notice when you change PMR providers?") is one of the most powerful posts in the recent window: 3,696 impressions, 68 reactions, 7 comments, 3 reposts, 2,700 clicks, 75.16 percent engagement rate. The post opens on a genuine reader question (what changes when you switch), uses Khal by name, references the specific site count (four), and closes with "site one" leading into the story arc. This is the four-ingredient recipe executed cleanly. The follow-up work (site-by-site breakdowns, Khal quote cards, the visit gallery) has not been shipped as of 10 Jul. It should ship in the next two weeks or the arc will fade.

### 6.3 Marton Road (Jaya Sai Vamsi Reddy Authunuri)

The 2026-05-08 single-image ("The tools Titan has built support the whole pharmacy ecosystem") is the top comment-earner of the recent window (10 comments) and delivered 88.8 composite score (`titan-voice-canon.md` entry 1). The Jul 24 Innovation Day announcement carousel (2026-06-30: "If you're building the next version of your pharmacy") drove 27 reactions, 7 comments, and 14 reposts (the highest repost count on any recent post). The 2026-07-10 video ("Modern community pharmacy isn't about one thing") drove 37 reactions, 7 comments, 8 reposts. This is a live campaign, well-executed, with the event date (24 Jul) still ahead. The playbook to follow now is: keep two more pre-event posts scheduled, capture the day for the five-role package (per the pharmacy-visit capture playbook), publish the post-event cluster within seven days.

### 6.4 Priory (Prabjaudt Channa)

The 2026-02-24 Prab quote card ("I was ready to leave pharmacy") generated 2,573 impressions, 49 reactions, **12 comments** (the second-highest of the review window), 4 reposts. That single line touched a professional identity nerve. The Priory Longform Video (2025-07-02, 6,728 impressions, 23.2 QES) remains the highest-reach organic customer story ever produced for the brand. The Prab archive is deep (8 posts, 27,438 impressions cumulative) and still under-exploited, the "Ready to leave pharmacy" line should be reproduced as a hero visual, and the 16,000-items-a-month scale number needs its own quote card.

### 6.5 BD Rowa / Balance Street (Innovation Night, 24 June)

The 2026-06-26 multi-image ("This pharmacy dispenses 21,000 items a month") delivered 2,477 impressions, 50 reactions, 6 reposts, 78.32 percent engagement rate. The post opened on the number, then landed the counterintuitive observation ("the picking column is empty. So is the checking column"). This is the reverse Geoff Malpas pattern, calm as headline, volume as evidence, and it works. The 2026-06-10 setup post ("For years, Titan PMR and BD Rowa have worked side by side") did the ecosystem framing job at 790 impressions and 5 reposts. This partnership event was smaller in scale than TitanUp 26 but proportionally sharper in content yield.

### 6.6 Consistent winners across all campaigns

Four ingredients repeat: (a) real named person, first name plus location, (b) specific scale number in the hook or within the first four lines, (c) counterintuitive observation or honest friction (the calm not the volume, the hesitation not the certainty), (d) minimal product mention until the reader's world is fully real. When any one is missing, performance drops sharply. When any two are missing, the post underperforms even the type median. This is the finding to build the H2 2026 calendar around.

## 9. Format, cadence and timing patterns

**Publication cadence.** Titan PMR ran at ~17 posts a month across Feb-Sep 2025, spiked to 27-30 in May-Jun 2026, dropped back to 9 in the first ten days of July. The volume-vs-reach curve is unmistakable: as posts per month rose from 17 to 30, average impressions per post fell from 2,758 (Nov 2025 baseline peak) to 900 (May 2026). Correlation is not causation, but the pattern matches Pulizzi's principle ("volume decreases as the audience matures") and the LinkedIn algorithm's tendency to throttle high-volume publishers.

**Day of week.** Wednesday leads on both reach and comments (80 posts, 1,447 avg impressions, 146 comments). Tuesday second (71 posts, 1,384 avg impressions, 134 comments). Thursday third (66 posts, 1,360, and highest total reposts at 254). Monday under-samples (42 posts, 1,401 avg). Friday sits below on reach (1,202) but comment count holds (133). Saturday and Sunday under-sample but reach is not markedly worse (1,218 and 1,588 respectively).

**Format mix by day.** No strong pattern in the data; format is chosen by content type, not by day. This is fine.

**Timing implication.** Move the top-priority posts of the week to Wednesday. Reserve Thursday for reposted or repost-optimised content (the day already over-indexes on advocacy). Reduce Friday count without dropping to zero (comments still fire, but reach drops). Skip Saturday and Sunday except for genuine timely content.

**Campaign arc timing.** Pharmacy Show 2025 and TitanUp 26 both proved that a two-week pre-event build plus a two-week post-event exploitation window is the right cadence. Innovation Day 24 Jul should sit in that window right now.

## 10. Trust vs vanity: what's actually moving the needle

Three signals move the needle:

**Comments.** These are the hardest to earn and the strongest signal of resonance. The comment leaderboard is dominated by named-operator emotional posts (Prab, Jaya, Sagar), founder direct voice (Sajid HOD twice, Tariq keynote), and milestone moments (1000th, Pharmacy Show announcement). This is the trust column.

**Reposts.** These are the strongest signal of utility. If someone reposts, they are attaching their name to your idea in their feed. The repost leaderboard is dominated by policy-with-stance (Stop Nomination, GTIN, apprenticeship), event coverage (Pharmacy Show, TitanUp), and curiosity teasers (Scan Me, QR Teaser). This is the utility column.

**Follows.** Only 12 follows across the 40-post Feb-Apr 2026 window analysed. The QR Teaser drove 4. The rest averaged less than 0.5 per post. This is the growth column, and it is largely not moving, a structural signal, not a content signal.

Two signals are vanity in this dataset:

**Impressions.** Carousel-swipe inflation makes engagement rate non-comparable across formats. Boosted posts distort the absolute numbers. Impressions are useful as a floor check (did the post reach anyone) but not as a ranking metric.

**Reactions alone.** Reactions without comments or reposts are the easiest signal to earn (one tap) and least informative. Sunny numbers on posts with zero comments and zero reposts should be treated as brand-noise, not trust.

The reporting rule is: rank by QES; sanity-check with comments and reposts as separate columns; treat impressions as a floor; ignore follow count in weekly reporting (it moves quarterly at best).

## 12. Production process review + capture recommendations

**What the pharmacy-visit capture playbook already gets right.** The five-role framework (Hero, Proof, Insight, Ecosystem, Social Texture) is the correct spine. The 8-category standing photo list, the two-mic audio rule, the standing question set, and the 2-3 week rollout map are all directly derived from the posts that have worked (Marton Road, Balance Street, Lansdales, Malpas). Keep this as the operational bible.

**Recurring capture gaps to close.**

1. **Named operator's specific scale number in the interview.** Every visit interview should extract at least one specific number that can carry a single-image quote card and a hero-post subheading. Prab's 16,000 items, Sagar's 4x growth, Geoff's 40,000 items with calm, Jaya's 500 patients in four weeks, Balance Street's 21,000 items. If the interview ends without one, the deliverable list halves.

2. **The honesty beat.** Every visit should extract at least one line about hesitation, initial doubt, the switching decision, or something they got wrong before Titan. Jaya's "he didn't jump in straight away" is the exemplar. This is the credibility ingredient the voice canon consistently identifies. Ask this question explicitly in interviews.

3. **The workflow moment on camera.** The 30-45s single-workflow-moment clip is the audience-signal-aligned deliverable (47 percent of TitanUp poll voters want to see the system working, not hear about it). Every visit needs one basket moving through the board, one AI clinical check happening live, one robot picking, one MDS tray progression. Landscape 16:9, no narration.

4. **Two-mic audio on team conversations.** When a Titan person accompanies the visit, the mixed-edit format (per the Sajid Innovation Day cut) is the highest-yield hero deliverable. This depends entirely on audio quality. Every visit with a Titan team member present needs two mics, minimum.

5. **The exterior shot at start and end.** The narrative-bracket shot (arrival handshake + departure handshake) reads as reportage, not marketing. This is a low-effort capture with disproportionate use in edit.

6. **The 30-second silent room tone recording.** Consistent request across every professional edit. Missing this can compromise the final cut.

**Structural mistakes to stop.**

- Filming without a five-role plan (produces one post from a visit day instead of three to five).
- Shooting the interview last instead of first (loses the number and the honesty beat because the interview is rushed).
- Not confirming spelling and titles for lower-thirds before leaving.
- Restating owner claims about competitors in brand voice (creates unnecessary legal exposure). Attribute to them.
- Skipping ambient B-roll (limits edit flexibility for the multi-image and hero post).

**Mandatory on every shoot.**

- Consent recorded on camera.
- Names and titles confirmed in writing before leaving the pharmacy.
- The five-role plan agreed with the Titan team member (if present) before filming starts.
- The named-scale number captured in the interview.
- The honesty beat captured in the interview.
- Landscape 16:9 for the interview.
- Two-mic audio if any conversation is filmed.
- Room tone recorded.
- Exterior, hero portrait, dispensary wide, screen closeup, team candid, details, arrival handshake, departure handshake, all photographed.
- One workflow-moment clip captured.

**Structuring one filming day to maximise multi-week content output.**

09:00 arrival, exterior shots, arrival handshake, wide dispensary photos (dispensary is quietest early). 09:30-10:30 owner interview in a quiet room with two mics, five-question set, extract number and honesty beat first. 10:30-11:30 workflow-moment clips, screen closeups, team candids while the pharmacy is running. 11:30-12:00 Titan team member walk-and-talk (car park works), captures fresh reactions. 12:00-12:30 lunch or informal conversations captured lightly for social texture. 12:30-14:00 second interview if there is a second story angle (a manager, a dispenser, a longtime patient). 14:00 departure handshake, group shot, farewell.

Total: five hours captured, delivering roughly:

- 1 hero post (multi-image or 60-90s video).
- 1 proof post (owner talking to camera, 60s cut).
- 1 insight post (single-image quote card or short video).
- 1 ecosystem post (workflow moment or hardware/software combined visual).
- 2-3 social texture posts (Instagram stories, Facebook casual, short TikTok cut).

Five days of visit capture per quarter, executed at that yield, produces 25-30 pieces of high-signal content per quarter, which is roughly two-thirds of the Titan PMR monthly cadence.

## 14. What to stop doing: the kill list

**Stop boosting cold-audience event promos.** Boosted posts averaged 0.1-1.1 percent engagement rate against 25 percent organic. Every pound spent on this in the last review window returned essentially nothing. If boosting must happen, boost proven organic winners (Prab quote card, Jaya "the tools", Malpas gallery), never event promos to cold audiences.

**Stop the generic "one platform" positioning.** The library has 12 different captions saying essentially the same thing. The audience has heard it. Every new post needs a new customer, a new number, a new integration, or a new angle. Reframe or drop.

**Stop shipping customer transformation as short clips.** 19.8 average QES on customer_transformation is a shipping-format failure, not a content-type failure. Multi-image, carousel, and longform video work. Short clips lose the numbers, the arc, and the honesty beat.

**Stop back-to-back post-type repetition.** 51 violations in the compliance analysis. Two customer transformation posts in a row equals audience fatigue. Two feature posts in a row equals audience fatigue. The formula rule exists for a measured reason.

**Stop featuring the same customer within a three-week window.** 61 rotation violations. Rahul has 18 posts and 24 days since last feature as of 10 Jul. Prab, Sagar, Yusuf and Rahul between them carry most of the customer content. The library is deeper than that.

**Stop LinkedIn-terminal CTAs on the majority of posts.** The rung-five default is a habit, not a strategy. Every Wednesday and Thursday CTA should point at an owned destination.

**Stop the engagement question format.** 4 posts, 11.1 average QES, the worst of any classified type. Community-builder in theory; low-effort in practice; the audience does not treat it as an invitation. Kill or fully rethink.

**Stop the short loop videos without narrative.** Three consecutive loops killed the middle of the TitanUp campaign. Loops work as feature-in-motion only when paired with a voice, a customer, or a specific number. Ambient brand loops are pure filler.

**Stop the duplicate posting across Notionsocial and organic.** Same content posted twice with near-identical captions dilutes reach without adding reach. Pick one channel per post.

**Stop describing COMING features as LIVE.** Wahid's LIVE / COMING / FUTURE distinction (`titanup-26-announcements.md`) is now the fact-discipline standard. Any post that violates it is a credibility failure in the most visible content lane.
