# Titan Content Strategy

The multi-channel strategic reference for Titan content. Replaces `linkedin-content-strategy-report.md`. Covers LinkedIn, TikTok, blog, YouTube, written case studies, email (when launched), TitanUp, and titanup.club, all operating into the unified tilt as one Content Inc engine.

**Reading order:** `titan-content-foundations.md` first (the strategic anchor), then this file (channel-by-channel tactical direction), then `titan-content-voice-guide.md` (voice and craft execution).

**Status:** v1. LinkedIn strategy is evidence-based (dataset-derived). TikTok, blog, YouTube, case study, and email strategies are strategic plan pending evidence accumulation. Updated as evidence comes in.

---

## 1. The strategic frame

The unified tilt is *"Titan is the AI-powered operating stack for UK pharmacy entrepreneurs growing from items to services — protecting margin and team time on the way."*

The four-layer stack — Destination → Dispensary → Clinical Platform → Shopfront — is the brand architecture. PMR is the Dispensary layer; Titanverse is the Clinical Platform layer; PharmAppy is the Shopfront layer.

The customer is the **willingness-to-adapt operator** — UK pharmacy entrepreneurs who have already decided to change. Not all UK pharmacies; not the size criterion; the willingness is the screening criterion.

Every channel in this strategy publishes into the same tilt. Format, hook, cadence, and angle adapt to the channel and to where the audience sits in the stack. The medium changes the shape; the tilt does not.

---

## 2. The Content Inc operating principles applied to Titan

From Pulizzi's framework, the principles that drive channel-level decisions:

1. **Build the base before diversifying.** LinkedIn is the strongest evidenced digital base. Don't pull resources to weaker bases until LinkedIn is sturdy.
2. **Subscribers over followers.** Every channel asks: where does this content drive owned audience capture? Rented platforms (LinkedIn, TikTok) exist to feed owned destinations (email, titanup.club, the blog).
3. **Niche before broaden.** Resist content that targets pharmacy generally. Target the willingness-to-adapt entrepreneurial operator specifically.
4. **Consistency beats volume.** Cadence holds; format mix holds; tilt holds. Pulizzi: volume *decreases* as the audience matures.
5. **Founder-led plays beat brand-led plays.** Tariq direct content outperforms Tariq retold content. Brand-voice content has a lower ceiling than founder-voice content.
6. **Ripples count.** Speaking invitations, press, partnership inbound, recruitment ripples all count as Content Inc returns. Track them.
7. **Media company that happens to sell.** Every piece of content should pass the test: strip the product mention, does it still have value? If no, it's marketing not content.

## 3. Channel-by-channel strategy

### 3.1 LinkedIn — Titan PMR page

**Status:** evidence-based. Dataset: 292 posts, Dec 2024 – May 2026. Approaching sturdy as the Pulizzi base.

**Stack position:** publishes from the Dispensary layer. Should connect upward (Destination — where is this customer going?) and across (the foundation for the Clinical Platform).

**Audience:** B2B operators, multi-site owners, sector decision-makers. Pharmacy managers and dispensing leads where they're decision-makers in their own right.

**Native job:** strategic frameworks, named-operator case studies, Tariq stance posts, policy-to-practice carousels, brand-defining declarative statements.

**Evidence-based winning clusters** (originally from comments-weighted top-quartile analysis; that method was retired July 2026 in favour of the real-engagement composite in `titan-voice-canon.md` — clusters stand as findings, flagged for re-validation at the next canon re-curation):

1. **Named operator + specific scale number + transformation arc.** Dominant winning pattern across formats. Sagar 4x, Brighton Hill 4k→17k, BMP 170k, Priory 16k, Howletts, Medichem hub-and-spoke, Geoff Malpas 40k items. *Named operator + specific items number + before/after.*
2. **Policy-to-practice with a stance.** Pharmacy contract changes, Pharmacy First walk-away, bulk nomination, Mounjaro pricing, GTIN safety. *Specific regulatory/policy shift + taking a side competitors won't.*
3. **Brand-defining declarative statements.** "Titan checks every script", "Pharmacy didn't need another PMR. It needed a new way of working", "Your pharmacy isn't struggling. Your PMR is." *Short declaratives that reframe the category.*
4. **Tariq founder voice, substantive moments only.** Keynote talk, panel data video. *Tariq direct works; Tariq retold doesn't.*

**Evidence-based anti-tilt clusters** (reach but zero comments):
- Short customer story clips (longform versions of the same stories win)
- Workflow demonstrations and feature tutorials (Faz & Nav demo, Batch Flow dispenser testimonial, Kanav workflow)
- Brand-cultural content (merch, BBQ, head-office-day, generic seasonal moments)
- Polls and explicit engagement-bait

**Cadence:** 4 posts per week. Front-load Mon-Thu. Friday lighter or skipped unless timely news.
- Mon: carousel
- Tue: video
- Wed: carousel / multi-image
- Thu: meme / single-image / loop
- Fri: skip

**Format mix priorities:**
- Carousels lead on reach + ER (median reach 744, max 3,065). Default lever for engagement.
- Videos lead on comments (median 1.0, max 15 on Tariq keynote). Default lever for conversation.
- Images lead reach when policy/stance content (GTIN at 4,501 reach, highest in dataset). Best for declarative statements and stance content.

**Subscriber routing:**
- Currently primarily building at rung 5 (LinkedIn Connections). Above only Facebook Fans in Pulizzi's hierarchy.
- Every post should do one of: build rung 5 (rented audience) OR convert rung 5 to rungs 1-4 (owned). If a post does neither, it's filler.
- The owned destination is titanup.club (verified community) and the email list (when launched). Route Wednesdays and Thursdays deliberately to owned destinations.

**What to test:**
- Tariq direct posts (not retold) on his own LinkedIn profile, cross-posted to Titan PMR
- Tariq analogies (bricklayer, dentist, Tesla, Amazon, boiling frog, skyscraper) as standalone carousels
- Polarising stance posts using Tariq's published print stances
- Owned-destination routing as primary CTA (currently 17/20 posts terminate on LinkedIn)

### 3.2 LinkedIn — Titanverse page

**Status:** evidence-based but smaller sample (65 posts, Oct 2025 – Apr 2026). Directional findings, sample size caveats apply.

**Stack position:** publishes from the Clinical Platform layer. Should connect upward (Destination — items-to-services thesis) and downward (the PMR foundation it sits on).

**Audience:** pharmacy operators running or scaling services, multi-site owners moving items-to-services, services-first pharmacies, prescribers in pharmacy settings.

**Native job:** business-model shift framing, services-as-revenue case studies, prescribing flow content, fragmentation-to-flow positioning.

**Evidence-based winning clusters:**

1. **Business-model shift framing.** "Two pharmacies, same building, different future" (cm 7 — top of dataset), "Pharmacy is shifting fast. Money is in services", "From dispensing to health hub". Dominant winning lane.
2. **Feature loops with fragmentation-resolution framing.** "Seamless Integrations", "Guided Clinical Consultations", "One place for services. No faff. No noise." Features framed as resolving fragmentation, not as standalone capabilities.
3. **Launch momentum** (time-bounded). Countdown content, webinar thanks. Useful for event-anchored campaigns; doesn't generalise to ongoing publishing.

**Evidence-based anti-tilt clusters:**
- Operational/feature posts that sit between PMR and TV (EPS nomination, Central NMS delivery)
- Short customer clips (same pattern as PMR)
- Disconnected quote images (high reach, no conversation)
- Tariq derivative content (Tariq hype videos die; Tariq direct works)

**Cadence:** 3-4 posts per week.
- Mon: carousel / multi-image
- Tue: video / loop
- Wed: single-image
- Thu: video / carousel
- Fri: skip

**Format mix priorities:**
- Image lead on reach for TV currently (median 560, max 1,213). Single-image business-model framing is the highest-reach format.
- Video for feature loops and customer stories.
- Carousel underused at 10 posts in 7 months. Should increase share — carousels are the dual-layer 5.9-controlled scarce slot but TV is under-using its share.

**Subscriber routing:**
- TV-specific email list and dedicated community destination both currently underdefined.
- titanup.club is shared with PMR. Whether TV needs a separate owned destination is an open strategic question.

**What to test:**
- Direct application of Tariq's dental analogy as TV carousel content
- Tariq's June 2024 Pharmacy Survival Guide six-point framework as a TV-led series
- More explicit cross-layer references to PMR (the dispensary foundation that TV sits on)
- TV-specific email signup as Wednesday/Thursday primary CTA

### 3.3 TikTok

**Status:** strategic plan, evidence-gathering needed. Cam's read is that TikTok is one of Titan's strongest-engagement channels but the dataset to verify hasn't been built into this project yet.

**Stack position:** publishes from across the stack but optimised for the Dispensary and Clinical Platform layers. The Shopfront layer (PharmAppy) is the natural fit for TikTok but currently treated lightly.

**Audience:** broader pharmacy workforce — operators, pharmacists, dispensers, preregs, students. The audience is wider than LinkedIn's B2B-decision-maker focus and skews younger.

**Native job:** the front-of-house view of the same stack story. Where LinkedIn shows the framework, TikTok shows the workflow moment. Day-in-the-life content. Customer surprise moments. Memes grounded in pharmacy reality. Recruitment-of-mindset content (people who would be drawn to working at a Titan pharmacy).

**Content lane proposals (pending evidence):**

1. **Workflow-in-action.** The audience says "show me exactly how it works" (47% of TitanUp poll voters). TikTok is the channel for this. 30-60 second clips of the system doing what it does. Pharmacy First consultation in one flow. Batch Flow processing baskets. AI clinical check happening in real time.
2. **Day-in-the-life.** Pharmacy team members showing their work. The willingness-to-adapt operator profile in motion. Authentic, low-production, character-driven.
3. **Customer surprise moments.** "Wait, you can do that now?" reactions to features. Captured live or near-live at customer visits.
4. **Memes.** Pharmacy life recognisable across roles. Light, but grounded. No "we're trying to be cool" energy.
5. **Tariq direct.** Short Tariq talks captured at events, customer visits, or office moments. The analogies as 60-second TikTok scripts.

**Cadence proposal:** to be set once evidence accumulates. Pulizzi's principle: pick a cadence and hold it. Don't post randomly. Don't double-down before knowing what's working.

**Cross-channel reuse:**
- LinkedIn carousel → TikTok script breakdown (one TikTok per slide if the carousel has 4-6 slides)
- LinkedIn longform customer video → TikTok short cuts (but LinkedIn evidence says these underperform on LinkedIn — TikTok may be where the short cuts find their natural home)
- Tariq analogies as carousels on LinkedIn + as 60-second TikToks for the broader audience

**What to build first:**
- TikTok performance dataset structured the same way as the LinkedIn JSONs, so dataset-led analysis becomes possible
- An explicit format taxonomy (workflow demo / day-in-life / customer reaction / meme / Tariq direct)
- A 4-week consistent cadence test with one format per day

### 3.4 Blog (titanpmr.com / titanverse.com)

**Status:** strategic plan, currently underused. The blog should become the canonical home for Tariq's longform thinking on the Titan domain.

**Stack position:** the persistent, search-indexable, linkable version of the unified tilt. Where LinkedIn drives conversation in 600-character bursts, the blog hosts the 2,000-word thesis.

**Audience:** search-driven traffic (pharmacy operators researching specific topics), returning readers, linkable assets for press and sponsor partnerships.

**Native job:**
- Longform versions of Tariq's frameworks (Survival Guide, Destination Journey, the analogies)
- Canonical home for Tariq's Pharmacy Business column on the Titan domain
- Linkable strategic assets that LinkedIn posts can point to
- SEO ranking for the specific tilt keywords ("AI pharmacy operating stack", "items to services pharmacy", "UK pharmacy entrepreneur software")

**Content lane proposals:**

1. **Tariq's column as the lead blog content.** Each new monthly Pharmacy Business column gets a canonical Titan-domain version published in the same week. Pharmacy Business gets the magazine version; titanpmr.com gets the durable searchable version. Same content, different home.
2. **The framework library.** Each of Tariq's published frameworks (Six Pillars 2020, Survival Guide 2024, Crossroads 2024, Destination Journey 2026) gets a dedicated long-form blog page. Each becomes a permanent linkable asset.
3. **The analogy library.** Each of the six signature analogies (bricklayer, dentist, Tesla, Amazon, boiling frog, skyscraper) gets a long-form explainer page. Each is a linkable asset that LinkedIn posts can deep-link to.
4. **Named customer case studies (long version).** The longform customer story written up at length, beyond the LinkedIn carousel and the YouTube video. With named operator, specific items numbers, transformation arc, photos, video embed.
5. **Policy reactives in long form.** When Tariq has a stance on a regulatory shift, the blog hosts the full essay version. LinkedIn carries the carousel summary; the blog carries the full argument.

**Cadence proposal:** at minimum one substantial new post per month (the Tariq column reproduction). Additional posts as customer stories and policy moments arise.

**Cross-channel role:**
- The blog is the long memory of the Content Inc engine. LinkedIn is the rented base; the blog is the owned long memory.
- Email (when launched) links to blog posts as the canonical version.
- LinkedIn posts can deep-link to blog explainers for the full argument.

### 3.5 YouTube

**Status:** strategic plan, currently used for case study archive but not as an active Content Inc leg.

**Stack position:** the durable video version of the unified tilt. Where LinkedIn delivers the 60-second cut, YouTube hosts the 8-minute longform.

**Audience:** operators researching Titan specifically, returning case-study viewers, prospect deep-dive.

**Native job:**
- Longform customer stories as durable assets (the Brighton Hill, BMP, Priory, Howletts, Medichem stories in their full forms)
- Framework explainer videos (Tariq presenting the four-layer stack, the dental analogy)
- Founder talks captured at events
- Demo videos showing the system in extended action (responding to the TitanUp signal: 47% want live demos)

**Content lane proposals:**

1. **Longform customer story films.** 5-10 minutes per customer. Named operator, transformation arc, specific numbers, real workflow footage. CMotionDesign's documentary production capability is the production engine.

**Cross-channel role:**

### 3.7 Email (when launched)

**Status:** does not exist yet. The biggest single Content Inc opportunity available to Titan.

**Stack position:** rung 1 of Pulizzi's Subscription Hierarchy — the most owned audience layer available. Currently absent from Titan's operation.

**Why this is the priority:** Pulizzi: *"Content Inc leaders should consistently keep one metric top-of-mind: subscriber growth. Email subscribers are at the top of the subscription hierarchy because businesses have the most control over email."* Titan is currently optimising for rung 5 (LinkedIn) and has no rung 1 leg. Launching email is the single largest move on the table.

**Audience:** owned subscribers — people who have actively opted in to hear from Titan. Mostly existing customers, lead-generation form fills, case study PDF downloads, TitanUp registrations.

**Native job:** the most direct, owned, monthly conversation with the audience. Cannot be throttled by algorithm. Read whenever the recipient chooses. The truest test of "does the audience actually want what we make."

**Content structure proposal:**

A monthly newsletter (or biweekly when the volume supports it) with three sections:

1. **Tariq's column** — the latest Pharmacy Business column reproduced, with a brief introduction from Tariq. The biggest single piece of original Titan thinking each month.
2. **The customer story of the month** — the named-operator-specific-transformation story from the latest customer film. Quote, key number, link to the full case study and the YouTube video.
3. **The framework drop** — one strategic framework, analogy, or stat. Could be from Tariq's archive (the bricklayer analogy, the 0.12% override rate) or new. Designed to be quotable in the operator's own conversations.

**Cadence:** monthly to start. Pulizzi's consistency principle. Hold for 12 months minimum before increasing frequency.

**Subscriber acquisition routes:**
- TitanUp registration (already exists — these are warm leads who haven't been re-engaged systematically)
- Case study PDF downloads (build the PDF gates first)
- LinkedIn post CTAs (Wednesdays/Thursdays specifically)
- titanup.club verified members (cross-pollination between the community and the email list)
- Conference sign-ups at Pharmacy Show and other events

**What to do first:** set up the email infrastructure (a Mailchimp, ConvertKit, or similar account, plus a clean acquisition form on titanpmr.com). Then ship the first three monthly issues before announcing widely. Test the format with a small audience before scaling.

## 4. Cross-channel cohesion principles

The unified tilt is the same everywhere. The medium changes the shape. Specific rules:

**Cohesion rule 1 — One story, four shapes.** A single customer story might appear as:
- A 5-minute YouTube longform (the durable canonical version)
- A LinkedIn longform video (4-6 minute version with caption framing)
- A LinkedIn carousel (6-8 slides extracting key beats)
- A 60-second TikTok (the workflow moment in front of camera)
- A written case study with PDF gate (the high-intent conversion version)
- An email feature (the monthly newsletter customer story section)
- A blog post (the durable searchable text version)

Same story, seven channel-native shapes. The shape changes; the named-operator-specific-scale-transformation framing does not.

**Cohesion rule 2 — Tariq's voice is consistent across channels.** When Tariq is the byline, the voice devices (analogies, frameworks, direct language) are the same on LinkedIn, in print, on the blog, in a TikTok, at TitanUp. Variation is in format, not in voice.

**Cohesion rule 3 — Cross-layer references are good; cross-brand confusion is not.** PMR posts referencing TV (and vice versa) reinforce the stack. PMR posts that read like TV content (or vice versa) confuse the audience. The test: would a reader know what layer they're in?

**Cohesion rule 4 — Owned destinations are the centre of gravity.** Every rented-channel post (LinkedIn, TikTok, blog) should be evaluated by what it does to owned audience (email, titanup.club, the case study email gate). The rented channels are top-of-funnel; owned is the asset.

**Cohesion rule 5 — Founder ripples accumulate cross-channel.** Tariq speaking at an event leads to LinkedIn coverage which leads to a YouTube upload which leads to a blog write-up which leads to email distribution which leads to titanup.club discussion. One source event, six channel touches. Track this explicitly.

## 5. The dual-layer 5.9 scheduling framework (LinkedIn-specific)

When planning weeks where both Titan PMR and Titanverse are publishing on LinkedIn:

- Classify every post as HEAVY, MEDIUM, or LIGHT before scheduling
- Never two HEAVY posts on the same day from the same layer's page
- Max one carousel per day across both layers
- Space carousels at least 24 hours apart
- Alternate which layer gets the heavy slot across the week
- Always check what's already scheduled before placing anything new

**Framing reminder:** PMR and TV are layers of one stack, not separate brands. Content scheduled into both should reinforce the stack architecture (cross-layer references are good), not run as parallel brand campaigns.

The 5.9 framework is LinkedIn-specific. Other channels have their own scheduling logic (TikTok cadence is tested per content lane; blog is at-least-monthly cadence; email is monthly; YouTube is at-least-monthly; TitanUp is annual; titanup.club is continuous).

## 8. Test queue (what we're still figuring out)

Deliberate experiments worth running. Each should run for at least 4 weeks to generate usable signal.

**Test 1 — Tariq direct on LinkedIn.** Hypothesis: Tariq posting on his own LinkedIn profile, cross-posted to Titan PMR, outperforms brand-voice posts on the same topic. Method: 1 Tariq direct post per fortnight for 8 weeks. Compare to brand-voice posts of comparable topic.

**Test 2 — Tariq analogies as standalone carousels.** Hypothesis: each of the six signature analogies generates engagement above the carousel median. Method: 1 analogy carousel per fortnight for 12 weeks (covers all six). Track comments and reach against baseline.

**Test 3 — Owned-destination routing as primary CTA.** Hypothesis: posts with owned-destination CTAs (titanup.club, email signup) drive measurable owned-audience growth without sacrificing reach.
