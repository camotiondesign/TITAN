# TitanUp 26 Announcements — Product Truth Addendum

Supplement to `titan-pmr-knowledge.json` and `titanverse-knowledge.json`, which predate TitanUp 26 (7 June 2026). Until those files are regenerated, **this file is the authority on everything announced on stage.** For anything not listed here, the knowledge JSONs remain authoritative.

**The critical discipline: availability status.** Wahid was explicit on stage about what is live versus coming. Content that implies a "coming" feature is available now is a fact-discipline failure in the highest-visibility lane we have. Every item below carries one of three flags:

- **LIVE** — available now, stated on stage.
- **COMING (months)** — "in the next coming months... this year for sure" per Wahid. No firm dates were given; never invent one.
- **FUTURE** — Oracle wave one "by next year"; NHS integration timing explicitly uncertain.

---

## Titan AI Level 5 — LIVE

- Announced and launched on stage by Wahid. Complete ground-up rebuild, described as **agentic AI**: "many AI models that are running in sequence, specialised models that do a certain thing."
- Models built, trained and owned by Titan, running on Titan's own infrastructure and data centres. "This isn't ChatGPT, this isn't Claude."
- Every prescription "forensically analysed... checked against the BNF" and against "real live usage of community pharmacy data." Reads doctor's additional information and reconstructs dosage.
- **Performance: "upwards of 70% full automation at the moment." Prediction: "around 90% plus" within six to eight months** — the 90% is a stated prediction, not a current capability.
- No user training period required, unlike earlier levels: "the model's ready to go. You switch it on, you'll start hitting those numbers."
- Team credentials (citable): AI engineering led by Professor Mohammed Bilal plus two PhD-qualified AI engineers; internal clinical team trained the models on "hundreds of thousands of rows"; in-house solicitor (Melissa) on legal frameworks; aligning to MHRA medical-device classification; externally audited.
- **Supersedes the old fact-check flag:** Level 5 is now documented (the PMR knowledge JSON only went to Level 4). The 70% clinical-checking figure is first-party from Wahid on stage.

## Titanverse repivot — strategic, effective immediately

Wahid explicitly repositioned Titanverse: **"Titanverse is your business platform, it's your finance platform, and it is your business in your pocket."** Not just a private/clinical services platform. Content should lead this repivot (the website has not caught up as of July 2026). The four content-relevant faces:

1. Private services platform (established, LIVE)
2. NHS services platform (COMING — see below)
3. Business control platform (concessions, claims, rules — mixed LIVE/COMING)
4. Finance platform (gross profit, FP34 — COMING)

## Titanverse private services — LIVE

- Available "literally tonight": bookings, payments, pre-consults, AVT voice-transcribed consultations, doctor's letters, treatment plans, vitals capture, website integration.
- NMS included. Clinical checks from Titanverse across multiple branches included.
- PGDs: structured PGDs integrated, backed by partners at Allied ("the gold standard"), on-premises workflow — announced as in progress/rolling out; check status before claiming fully live.
- Telemedicine, video calling, subscriptions: in development (COMING).

## Titanverse business & finance tools — COMING (months), some elements demoed as built

Announced by Wahid as the current focus of development, "coming out in the next couple of months... this year for sure":

- **Concessions tracker** — tracks items likely to enter the concessions list, lets pharmacies hold back claims until prices land.
- **Claims management** — view, hold back (concessions, specials, custom lists), endorsement completeness.
- **Equalisation deals & pack optimisation** — enforce brand/drug scanning rules centrally, blocked at the dispensary in real time.
- **Expiring items view.**
- **Real-time gross profit** — BSA pricing engine reverse-engineered (the Newcastle trip); cost of goods via integrations with drug comparison and eCass ordering. "Was today a good day? Now you can answer it."
- **FP34 analysis** — every claim tracked through Titan's engine; identifies missed endorsements ("You just lost out on £1,000. We can do that deep analysis now.").
- **Titan Business App (mobile)** — Android and iOS: multi-site command centre, custom KPIs with red/amber/green alerts, clinical checking from the phone, consultation extension (photos, signatures, payments), messaging across the platform and into Titan itself.

## Titanverse NHS services — FUTURE (timing explicitly uncertain)

- Titan selected as one of very few IT suppliers on the NHS assured-supplier programme for NYS/BaRS integration (contraception, hypertension, Pharmacy First, GP Connect).
- Deep integration, not a bolt-on: NHS-to-private gateway interchange is the design goal. Commitment stated to independent prescribing and RABS integration.
- Wahid, verbatim: "The only big question mark is the NHS side of things... I can't give an exact date on that." **Never put a date on NHS features.**

## Oracle — FUTURE (first wave "by next year", i.e. 2027)

The closing reveal. "Perhaps the most important product that we will ever make" (Tariq).

- AI agents sitting inside the pharmacy's data; natural-language questions replace reports: "There is no need for reports. You simply talk."
- Reaches beyond the PMR into finance systems (Xero, QuickBooks) and HR systems.
- Demoed on stage: profit by patient, profit by tag, delivery-route loss analysis, staff-performance dashboards built on request, dashboards pushed to TV screens and the business app, bulk actions ("send an SMS to all patients who haven't collected in five days" — 142 texts sent live), self-built agents (a monthly NHS-services sales agent created with a couple of prompts), voice agents inbound and outbound, multilingual (Urdu, Gujarati and others).
- Guardrails stated as built in.
- Wahid's forward claim: "in the next 12 to 18 months, most of the PMR stuff that you're doing... will become redundant and replaced with this kind of technology." Present as his stated direction, not a shipped fact.
- **Content rule: Oracle is a vision-lane subject only.** Every Oracle post must carry a future-tense frame. No availability, pricing or date claims exist beyond "first wave by next year."
