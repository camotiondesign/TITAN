## The thesis in one line

Titan doesn't need another advert. Independent pharmacy needs a serious podcast where owners talk honestly about running their businesses — and Titan happens to be the company hosting it.

## Why podcast, why now

- **LinkedIn reach is throttling.** Q4 strategy explicitly moves budget toward channels we own outright.
- **Titan's positioning is "changes what deserves a pharmacist's attention."** The podcast is the biggest possible expression of that thesis — literally paying attention to real pharmacy owners for an hour, in their own words.
- **Wahid's Aug 4 direction:** cut through information overload. Podcast is the format audience returns to weekly, not just when the algorithm surfaces it.
- **New office basement enables production quality** that most competitors can't match. Fixed studio, controlled lighting, no travel for hosts.
- **Content spins out for months per episode.** One hour of recording generates: 1 full episode, 6-10 short-form clips, quote cards, LinkedIn text posts, and newsletter fodder. The most efficient content unit in the mix.

## Format

**Name (working):** *Diary of a Pharmacy Owner*
(Or: *The Pharmacy Owner Diaries* · *Running a Pharmacy* · *The Titan Diaries* — Wahid pick)

**Concept:** Genuine conversation with a UK independent pharmacy owner about the business of running a pharmacy today. Not a Titan product show. Not a features rundown. A "how do you actually do this" show that owners will send each other because it captures the real job.

**Style reference:** *Diary of a CEO* (Steven Bartlett) — long-form conversation, professional set, warm register, guest-first questions. Not softball. Owners talking honestly with a peer.

**Length:** 45-60 min episodes. Cut into 3-6 short-form vertical clips per episode for social distribution.

**Cadence:** Weekly at launch, dropping every Tuesday. Bank 4 episodes before Nov launch to buffer against production hiccups.

**Recording:** In-studio at new office basement, dedicated set. Guest travels to Titan HQ. Filmed 4K, multi-cam (3 cameras: A-cam guest, B-cam host, C-cam wide two-shot).

## Episode structure (60 min target)

1. **Cold open (0-3 min)** — guest tells one specific story from their week. Sets the tone: this is a working pharmacy, not a marketing conversation.
2. **How you got here (3-15 min)** — origin story. Family? Bought a shop? Started fresh? Where did the ambition come from?
3. **The real job today (15-35 min)** — how the business actually runs. What eats the day. What the team does well. What still breaks.
4. **Where you're going (35-50 min)** — the plan. Services expansion? Multi-site? Sale? Legacy?
5. **What most people get wrong (50-58 min)** — the piece of advice they'd give another owner. The strongest quotes come out of this section.
6. **Closer (58-60 min)** — one thing they wish the industry was talking about more.

- **Guest willingness signal:** unsolicited inbound guest requests by month 3 = signal the show has traction in the sector
- **Downstream conversion:** demo bookings mentioning "heard on the podcast" — needs Titan sales team to capture. Zero is a red flag by episode 20.
