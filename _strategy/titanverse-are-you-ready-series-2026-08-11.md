## Universal carousel skeleton

Every episode uses the same 7-slide shape. Only the topic-specific content varies. This is deliberate — the repeating rhythm is what makes the series recognisable.

- **Slide 1 (cover):** Two-line hook. First line = the trend. Second line = the "Is your pharmacy ready?" question. ARE YOU READY? · Ep N badge top-right.
- **Slide 2 (service framing):** Where this service sits in the growing services stack. Titanverse worldview reasserted.
- **Slide 3 (what that adds):** Reusable "Every new service adds:" four-bullet ✔ list. What operators feel, not what they should worry about.
- **Slide 4 (argument line):** Full-bleed statement, "Scaling [service] without the operating model underneath is how good pharmacies get themselves in trouble."
- **Slide 5 (pressure translated):** Five stacked questions in owner language, escalating typographic weight. Small line beneath: "The questions get louder as demand rises."
- **Slide 6 (what Titanverse does):** "That's where Titanverse comes in." Four ✔ features specific to the service.
- **Slide 7 (belief close):** Fixed template. "More services shouldn't mean more chaos." Divider. "Titanverse. The operating layer for pharmacy services."

# Series consistency notes

- **Fixed cover badge:** ARE YOU READY? · Ep N appears top-right of every episode cover. Build once, reuse.
- **Fixed sign-off template:** Slide 7 is templated. Build once, reuse.
- **Reusable "what that adds" slide (Slide 3):** Layout templated. Bullet content changes per episode.
- **Illustrated pharmacist character:** consistent across all episodes. Expression varies per slide context.
- **Hashtag pattern:** always three, always ends with #CommunityPharmacy where relevant. Middle tag is the service-specific one (#WeightManagement, #PharmacyFirst, #IndependentPrescribing, #FluSeason, #Diagnostics, #PharmacyServices).
- **Leader quote insertion (optional per episode):** Where a leader (Hooman, Wahid, Tariq) has a verbatim line that fits the topic, insert as "As Hooman put it: '...'" between Titanverse ✔ bullets and the sign-off. Only when the quote genuinely lands. Force nothing.

# Adaptations across formats

Each carousel has three natural derivatives:

- **Single-image belief card** using the episode's argument line. Ship the Tuesday before the carousel drops.
- **Short video (30-45 sec)** with Hooman or Wahid delivering the argument line to camera + on-screen bullet reveal of the Titanverse ✔ list. Ship the Friday after the carousel drops.
- **Reels/Shorts cutdown (15 sec)** of the argument line as a hook. Ship on TT / IG / YT Shorts the Sunday after.

Not every episode needs all three. Priority order: carousel → single-image belief card → short video → cutdown.
