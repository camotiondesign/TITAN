# Fast Company Tech Headlines, Riffed for Titan
*Source: fastcompany.com/technology, monthly inspiration seed. Captured 11 Aug 2026.*

## Section 1. Top 10 that transfer best

### 1. Enterprise AI doesn't need another app: it needs its language
**What makes it work:** negation-plus-reframe. Names the wrong answer everyone assumes, then relocates the real one. The reader can't finish the line without inheriting the argument.
**Titan PMR riff:** "Pharmacy doesn't need another PMR. It needs the language its owners actually use."
**Titanverse riff:** "Community pharmacy doesn't need another consultation form. It needs a system that speaks the prescriber's day."
**Format:** LinkedIn single-image, both. IG carousel version if the Titanverse one turns into a language-vs-form comparison.

### 2. You trained the AI. Big Tech got paid.
**What makes it work:** two-clause reversal. The reader is cast as the labour, the villain is named in three words. Zero adjectives.
**Titan PMR riff:** "You built the patient list. Your PMR kept the data."
**Titanverse riff:** "You did the consultation. Your notes system got the value."
**Format:** LinkedIn single-image (bold statement card). IG single-image works too. Not a video; the punch is on the page.

### 3. The White House is fixated on China copying U.S. AI. Experts say that's the wrong threat.
**What makes it work:** authority reframe. Names the loud consensus, then trumps it with a counter-authority in the same sentence. Sets up a payoff the caption has to deliver.
**Titan PMR riff:** "The sector is fixated on hub and spoke going live. Owners running the numbers say that's the wrong bottleneck."
**Titanverse riff:** "The sector is fixated on the IP register. The pathfinder data says that's the wrong bottleneck." (echoes canon TV #6)
**Format:** LinkedIn carousel. The reframe is the cover, the sourced numbers do the rest.

### 4. ChatGPT's traffic surge is good news for brands. For publishers, it's complicated.
**What makes it work:** split-audience frame. Same event, two verdicts, one sentence. Forces the reader to work out which side they're on.
**Titan PMR riff:** "Hub and spoke is good news for pharmacy owners. For locum agencies, it's complicated."
**Titanverse riff:** "The IP register change is good news for pharmacists. For pharmacy owners without the software to prove it, it's complicated."
**Format:** LinkedIn carousel. Two-column cover slide, then split the argument. Also works as an IG carousel with left/right split.

### 5. The future of communication has nothing to do with phones. This is what's coming next.
**What makes it work:** false-negation reveal plus open loop. Contradicts the obvious answer, promises a specific replacement in the next line.
**Titan PMR riff:** "The future of dispensing has nothing to do with the dispensary." (leads to hub, robots, patient app taking the phones)
**Titanverse riff:** "The future of clinical services has nothing to do with the consultation." (follow-up, pathway, retention are where the value lives, echoes canon TV #3)
**Format:** LinkedIn video teaser (TED two-sentence shape) for both. Payoff held back to the film.

### 6. I told Mark Cuban he was wrong about AI. Here's what founders can learn from my mistake.
**What makes it work:** the honesty beat. The teller is the loser of the argument. That is more credible than any expert claim.
**Titan PMR riff:** "I told an owner AI clinical checks were a gimmick. Then I watched his pharmacist finish at 5 for the first time in six years."
**Titanverse riff:** "I told a pharmacist consultation transcription was a nice-to-have. Then Rahul showed me his notes from before."
**Format:** LinkedIn single-image with a longer caption (600 to 1500), the "I got this wrong" arc as the body. Sajid or Wahid voice.

### 7. Zynga's founder shares 6 mindsets for building billion-dollar products.
**What makes it work:** named authority plus numbered promise. The count sets the skim, the byline sets the trust.
**Titan PMR riff:** "6 mindsets separating pharmacies scaling services from pharmacies stuck on items." (source: Rahul Puri, TitanUp 26)
**Titanverse riff:** "6 things Rahul Puri does differently on private pathways than most contractors."
**Format:** LinkedIn carousel, both. Cover is the byline, six slides, close on the operator's own line ("don't die chasing volume").

### 8. Your smartphone is changing your body. Doctors say most people don't know it's happening.
**What makes it work:** unnoticed-change frame plus expert authority. Threat is personal, invisible, and endorsed by a category the reader already trusts.
**Titan PMR riff:** "Your item count is changing your patient list. Most owners don't notice until 1,000 items have quietly left." (Mukarrom's patient-drift quote)
**Titanverse riff:** "Your consultation workflow is changing your patients. Most pharmacists don't notice until the review never gets booked."
**Format:** LinkedIn carousel (data-led) or IG single-image with a single stat. This is a stakes-first mechanism explainer at heart.

### 9. With AI, we're all the sorcerer's apprentice.
**What makes it work:** literary allusion doing the whole warning in six words. Compresses a whole essay into a single image.
**Titan PMR riff:** "With AI on the label screen, you're the sorcerer or the apprentice. Pick before it picks for you."
**Titanverse riff:** "With AI transcribing the consultation, the pharmacist is the sorcerer. The system is the apprentice."
**Format:** LinkedIn single-image, illustrated. Tariq-voice caption. Sits well as an analogy-led post (craft file section 4).

### 10. Mark Zuckerberg lays out Meta's AI vision in a 6,500-word essay: 6 things to know.
**What makes it work:** long-form authority plus a numbered shortcut. Signals depth, then buys the reader out of reading it.
**Titan PMR riff:** "Tariq's 3,800-word survival essay from 2024, in the 6 lines running pharmacies in 2026."
**Titanverse riff:** "Ghulam Haidar's TitanUp 26 governance session in the 6 lines that would stop a fitness-to-practice case."
**Format:** LinkedIn carousel. Cover: the byline plus the count. Six pillar slides. Close on the source.

## Section 3. Discarded

- **Frankenstein has left the laboratory.** Too cute, no transfer.
- **ByteDance pulls the plug on AI companion services in China.** Audience-mismatch, off-tilt.
- **InKind $414m from Citi.** Financing frame, off-editorial.
- **Can skeptics survive the misinformation age?** Abstract, no operator lane.
- **The Cold War legend of Romania's tank-melting laser.** Pure curio, no bridge.
- **Inside the Trump admin $2b quantum push.** Political framing, off-tilt.
- **Trump's Head Start overhaul could deepen the digital divide.** US policy, no transfer.
- **College is getting even more expensive this fall.** Riff possible, insufficiently distinctive.
- **AI deepfakes are already in full force this election season.** Political, off-editorial.
- **AI startup Osmo wants to make corporate videos that don't look like slop.** Category insult with no natural pharmacy target.
- **Trump's AI investments turning Washington into a VC firm.** Political, off-editorial.
- **Where are Elon Musk's former DOGE staffers now?** Celebrity chase, off-editorial.
- **How Byron Allen plans to turn BuzzFeed into a YouTube rival.** Media rivalry, off-topic.
- **The AI boom has college majors dabbling in computer science.** No operator stakes.
- **Zuckerberg and Chan's school collapses.** Schadenfreude frame, off-tilt.
- **This fusion startup raised another $1b.** Sector-specific, no bridge.
- **1,900 U.S. public schools near a data center.** Proximity frame, forced in pharmacy.
- **Amazon limits access to reviews because it thinks they're AI.** Moderation frame, off-editorial.
- **New York sues Kalshi as illegal gambling.** Regulatory drama, off-topic.
- **EU cracks down on hacking and deepfakes with new team in Brussels.** EU policy, off-editorial.
- **YouTube and Substack are targeting AI slop.** Platform-cleanup frame, no clean map.
- **Run a free, private, offline AI on your computer.** Utility how-to, off-editorial.
- **Here's the live concert connection Spotify and YouTube Music are missing.** Media gap, forced in pharmacy.
- **5 ways AI could save our democracy.** Political salvation frame, off-tilt.
