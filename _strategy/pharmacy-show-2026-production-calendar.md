## Operating principle

> **Not every Titan post published during the Pharmacy Show runway is a Pharmacy Show campaign post.** Normal Titan publishing continues alongside the campaign. An asset only belongs in this calendar when its connection to the event is genuine. Avonnex Batch Flow (28 Aug) is the reference example: excellent Titan/partner workflow content, published in the runway, not a Pharmacy Show campaign asset.

## Slot definitions (audience function, per v5.1 §8.0)

- **Anchor:** advances the campaign argument. The piece the week revolves around.
- **Supporting:** supplies evidence, character or specificity that reinforces or extends the Anchor without repeating it.
- **Lightweight:** maintains rhythm, personality or participation without repeating the argument.

Production weight varies independently of slot. A designed single image can be an Anchor. A full-afternoon Reel shoot can be a Lightweight.

## Reason to care (per Titan Content Operating Strategy v3)

Every row is marker-tagged: **CRAFT** (how it's made) / **VALUE** (what the reader gets) / **CONNECTION** (who's in it) / **PROOF** (what it demonstrates).
