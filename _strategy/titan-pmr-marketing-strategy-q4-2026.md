## Summary

Two years of Titan PMR LinkedIn content generated 363 posts across 20 months. Independent analysis (blind, single dataset) grades the work **B+ on craft, C+ on outcomes**. The craft improved measurably — from feature-list brochure copy in early 2025 to observation-first customer-led storytelling by mid-2026. The outcomes did not compound — reach was flat or declining, follower growth unmeasured, downstream conversion untracked. That is the strategic problem this quarter fixes.

Q4 sets three things straight:
1. **Doubles down on what's proven to work** (named-operator case studies with hard before/after numbers, event saturation coverage, advocacy-with-action)
2. **Kills what has been shown not to work** (context-free one-liners, fourth-plus cuts on any case study, feature-listicles with no named human, unmeasured video volume)
3. **Instruments the four measurement blind spots** that make the C+ outcome grade currently unresolvable

Podcast (per Aug 4 meeting) launches as a new brand pillar in the November window, seeded by studio build-out in September.

## Section 1 — Analysis (honest read of the data)

### 1.1 What the numbers actually say

**Pipeline size:** 363 published LinkedIn posts on the Titan PMR page, Dec 16 2024 → Jul 31 2026.

**Year-on-year performance (outliers stripped):**

| Metric | 2025 (full) | 2026 (Jan-Jul) | Direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Median impressions per post | 1,321 | 854 | **↓ 35%** |
| Mean impressions per post | 1,667 | 1,114 | **↓ 33%** |
| Median real engagement rate | 5.39% | 6.87% | ↑ 27% |
| Total reactions | 4,337 | 2,814 | ↓ per-month roughly flat |
| Post volume per month | ~16 | ~20 | ↑ 25% |

The clear pattern: **per-post reach is down, per-post engagement rate is up.** Higher volume ate reach without compounding engagement. Total monthly engagement roughly flat.

### 1.2 What worked

Independent analysis and internal review converged on the same handful of formats:

- **Named-operator case studies with concrete before/after numbers.** Peak posts: Prabjaudt (Priory Pharmacy, 6,727 impressions, 12.49% real ER), Yusuf ("170,000 items a month sounds impressive, living it is something else" — 3,807 imp, 11.48%), Akbar (2,942 imp, 10.91%). These outperform every other format on both reach and engagement.
- **Event saturation coverage.** Pharmacy Show 2025 (Oct) and TitanUp 26 (Jun) both delivered the two best sustained content windows on record. Named-people wrap-up posts (Post 164, Post 320) consistently outperformed.
- **Advocacy with an action attached.** Highest share counts on record. "We were asked to build this functionality. We refused." (Post 203, 14 shares). Petition posts on funding/IP earned the most re-distribution.
- **Cultural/community posts** (Eid, Ramadan, Technician Day) achieved the highest engagement rates on the page — small reach but disproportionate warmth.
- **Named-milestone TEXT posts.** Top-performing post in 20 months: the 1,000th pharmacy live text post (4,785 impressions, 121 reactions, 50% real ER). Format proven for once-a-year use.

### 1.3 What didn't work

- **DOCUMENT format is heavily click-inflated.** LinkedIn counts carousel swipes as clicks, which shows 100%+ "engagement" on the dashboard. Real engagement (clicks stripped) averages 1.52% — the lowest of any format on the page. 69 documents got produced on the strength of a metric that wasn't measuring what it appeared to.
- **Context-free one-liner TEXT posts.** Six attempts, six failures (all under 1% real engagement).
- **Fourth-plus cuts on any case study.** Sagar's arc decayed 5,356 → 900 impressions across six cuts. Intra-story decay is the clearest single pattern in the dataset.
- **Feature-listicle posts with no named human.** The modal early-2025 format. Never once a top performer.
- **Blog-teaser reposts** (same URL 4-5 times per month). Reduced novelty, diminishing returns.
- **Unmeasured video.** 44% of all output is video. Retention was measured on 31 posts (Mar-Nov 2025), average watch time 12 seconds. 75 videos since Feb 2026 have zero retention data. The single largest resource commitment on the page is running without measurement.

### 1.4 Blind spots (measurement gaps)

Four things we don't currently instrument that prevent clean strategic decisions:

- **Follower count over time.** Without this we cannot separate "audience isn't growing" from "audience is growing but algorithmic reach is throttling." Two entirely different diagnoses, both plausible.
- **Paid/boost flags per post.** Post 103 (72,479 impressions, 21 reactions) and Post 214 (13,615 impressions, 0.12%) show the signature of paid boosts — but there's no flag. All averaged reach numbers are polluted by these.
- **Video retention on the 75 videos since Feb 2026.** Above.
- **Downstream conversion signal.** Zero linkage between content shipped and demo bookings, sign-ups, or contract closes. Without this, "did any of this sell software" — the question the budget hangs on — cannot be answered.

## Section 2 — Strategy (Q4 2026)

### 2.1 Framing

Quarterly cycle per Wahid's Aug 4 direction. Ship, measure, review, adjust — no long-horizon commitments. End of Oct: honest review against Q4 targets, then Q1 2027 plan issued.

### 2.2 The Q4 thesis in one line

**"Ship fewer posts, ship stronger posts, instrument what we ship, and build one owned distribution channel (podcast) that doesn't depend on LinkedIn's reach algorithm."**

### 2.3 Pillars to double down on

1. **Named-operator case study engine** — proven format, running through Butt Lane (Rahul, Stoke), Road Trip (Glevum/Parklands/Morrill), and continuing customer visits. Max 3 cuts per story, spaced over a month, never two siblings within four days.
2. **Hooman-led thought-leadership drumbeat** — the "Hub Question" campaign already scheduled Aug 4 → Sep 9 (11 posts). Extend as Sep monthly rhythm with Hooman COO Demos taking over Sep 1 onward.
3. **Event coverage saturation** — Pharmacy Show 2026 (October). Book coverage plan now. This is the proven best sustained window on the calendar.
4. **Advocacy-with-action** — currently under-used (2 posts in 20 months, both top-shared). One per month minimum for Q4.
5. **Cultural posts** — Diwali (November), Christmas / Titan Wrapped 2026. Warmth, not reach — set expectations accordingly.

### 2.4 Kill list

Already executed today (3 Aug):
- All Tariq leftover Idea/In Production posts (19)
- All Rahul Puri leftover keynote content (6)
- Weak TitanUp scatter quotes (5)
- Past-dated ProPharmace fillers (1)
- Wahid AutomationVsHub (no shoot happened) (1)
- Total: 32 posts moved to Archive

Ongoing kill rules for Q4:
- No context-free one-liner TEXT posts
- No repeat blog-teaser of the same URL within a month
- No fourth cut on any case-study arc
- No feature-listicle without a named human
- No generic "Titan is amazing" copy — replace with Idea-First Editorial framework (customer as proof, not premise)

### 2.5 Tests (each with success criteria)

Three tests, each with a clear yes/no answer at Q4 close:

1. **Founder-led distribution test.** Hooman personal profile reposts one hero post per week starting Sep 1. **Success:** ≥30% lift in impressions on those posts vs page-only baseline over 8 weeks.
2. **Volume rollback test.** Cut LinkedIn cadence to 3-4 posts/week (from ~5) outside event windows through Sep. **Success:** ≥20% lift in per-post reach on remaining posts.
3. **Video retention re-instrumentation.** Every video shipped Q4 has watch-time captured. **Success:** knowing by Q1 whether video at 44% output is earning its production cost. If avg watch time is still 12 seconds, video budget shifts to first-3-second discipline or lower share of output.

### 2.6 Podcast launch (see separate one-pager)

Podcast launches Nov 2026 as a new pillar. Studio build-out Sep. Recording begins Oct. First 4 episodes bank before public launch. Format: "Diary of a Pharmacy Owner" — guest-driven, industry-insight-focused, subtle Titan integration.

## Section 5 — What we'll measure

Quarterly cycle only works if we measure. Four numbers, monthly:

1. **Total impressions per platform** (LinkedIn + IG + FB + podcast download count once live)
2. **Median real engagement rate** (clicks stripped for DOCUMENT/MULTIIMAGE formats)
3. **Follower count** — snapshot first of every month, added to `analytics/monthly_snapshot.json`
4. **Boost/paid flag on any post** — captured at publish, kept out of averages

Plus one that requires Titan team involvement:

5. **Demo booking count per week, tagged to what content shipped that week** — the "did any of this sell software" question. Wahid help needed to establish this measurement loop with the sales team.
