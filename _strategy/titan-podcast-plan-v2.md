## The thesis in one line

Titan doesn't need another advert. Independent pharmacy needs a serious podcast where owners talk honestly about running their businesses — and Titan is the company that hosts it before anyone else does.

## Why podcast, why now

- **This space is unclaimed by any UK PMR competitor.** First-mover advantage evaporates fast. Own it in Q4 or lose the position permanently.
- **The one adjacent competitor is Pharmacy Planet** (outside PMR, community pharmacy adjacent). High follower count, silver-tier YouTube plate, but comments average roughly one per video — inflated views, low genuine engagement. Reputation in the industry: boxed. The bar to make something more real is not high.
- **Feature comparisons are commoditised now.** Anyone can ask ChatGPT or Claude "what's the difference between Titan PMR and PharmacyX" and get a coherent answer. What isn't commoditised: entertainment, insight, and a real conversation with someone the audience recognises. That's what brings people back weekly, not the feature list.
- **LinkedIn reach is throttling in 2026.** Q4 strategy explicitly moves budget toward channels Titan owns outright. Podcast is the first of those.
- **Titan's editorial positioning is "changes what deserves a pharmacist's attention."** A podcast that pays attention to real owners for an hour, in their own words, is the strongest possible expression of the thesis.
- **New office basement enables production quality most competitors can't match.** Fixed studio, controlled lighting, no travel for hosts.
- **One hour of recording = one week of content.** 1 full episode + 6-10 short-form clips + quote cards + LinkedIn text posts + newsletter fodder. Most efficient content unit in the mix.

## Format

**Working name:** *Diary of a Pharmacy Owner*
(Alternatives for consideration: *The Pharmacy Owner Diaries* · *Running a Pharmacy* · *The Titan Diaries*)

**Concept:** Genuine conversation with a UK independent pharmacy owner about the business of running a pharmacy today. Not a Titan product show. Not a feature rundown. A "how do you actually do this" show that owners will send to each other because it captures the real job.

**Style reference:** *Diary of a CEO* (Steven Bartlett) — long-form conversation, professional set, warm register, guest-first questions. Bartlett doesn't push products explicitly during the interview. His personal brand carries. Mid-roll ad slot is where the sponsor gets named. That's the mechanic.

**Titan integration mechanic:** roughly halfway through each episode, a 20-second sponsor read:

> *"This podcast is sponsored by Titan PMR — keeping your dispensary running and changing up the way pharmacy works with every innovation."*

Then straight back into the conversation. The rest of the episode is guest-first. Titan builds brand awareness without contaminating the conversation.

**Length:** 45-60 minutes per episode. Cut into 3-6 short vertical clips per episode for social distribution.

**Cadence:** Weekly at launch, dropping every Tuesday morning. Bank 4 episodes before Nov launch to buffer against production hiccups.

**Recording:** In-studio at new office basement, dedicated set. Guest travels to Titan HQ. Filmed 4K, multi-cam (3 cameras: A-cam guest, B-cam host, C-cam wide two-shot).

## Episode structure (60 min target)

1. **Cold open (0-3 min)** — guest tells one specific story from their week. Sets tone: this is a working pharmacy, not a marketing conversation.
2. **How you got here (3-15 min)** — origin story. Family business? Bought a shop? Started fresh? Where the ambition came from.
3. **The real job today (15-32 min)** — how the business actually runs. What eats the day. What the team does well. What still breaks.
4. **Mid-roll sponsor spot (32-32:30)** — 20 seconds. See mechanic above.
5. **Where you're going (32:30-50 min)** — the plan. Services expansion? Multi-site? Sale? Legacy?
6. **What most people get wrong (50-58 min)** — the piece of advice they'd give another owner. Strongest quotes come out of this section.
7. **Closer (58-60 min)** — one thing they wish the industry was talking about more.

## Success metrics (honest, first-year)

- **Episode downloads:** 300-800 per episode average by episode 12. Realistic for a niche B2B UK-only pharmacy show at launch. Pharmacy Planet's inflated view count is the wrong benchmark; the right benchmark is real comment count + sector conversation.
- **LinkedIn spinoff performance:** short-form clips outperform baseline Titan PMR video (currently 8.26% median real ER).
- **Guest willingness signal:** unsolicited inbound guest requests by month 3 = signal the show has traction in the sector.
- **Downstream conversion:** demo bookings mentioning "heard on the podcast" — needs Titan sales team to capture. Zero by episode 20 = red flag; force a diagnostic conversation.
