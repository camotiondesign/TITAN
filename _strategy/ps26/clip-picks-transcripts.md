## Notes & surprises

1. **Tariq/C0022.MP4 was not a short clip.** The brief expected a sub-90-second clip; it's actually a 29.8-minute, 21.8GB raw camera file of Tariq's Pharmacy Show conference keynote. Flagged to Cam mid-job; he asked to use local Whisper (faster-whisper, `base` model) instead of ElevenLabs Scribe to avoid the cost/time of transcribing 30 minutes through a paid API. Transcription completed locally in ~64 seconds. Content turned out to be a CEO keynote, not a customer testimonial, so it wasn't a fit for any of the three rows.
2. **Zad - 9.mp4 and Zad - 10.mp4 are duplicates** — identical transcript ("I mean, it looks like we're the only busiest stand here...") and near-identical file size (both 7,547,549 bytes). Worth checking on LaCie whether one is a stray duplicate export.
3. **Zad Video.mp4 (the main long edit) is a re-cut, not new material** — every usable line in it already exists in the individual numbered clips, so it wasn't treated as its own source. It also contains an unflagged competitor name ("cheaper than ProScript") in a section that doesn't map cleanly to any single one of the 18 exports — worth a manual re-check if Cam ever wants to pull from this edit directly.
4. **Two clips were excluded specifically for compliance/tone reasons:** Zad - 6 discloses a specific price figure (£550) mid-conversation, and Zad - 16 names a competitor product ("Victoria") plus contains profanity and cuts off mid-sentence. Neither is usable without heavy editing, so neither was picked.
5. **No competitor PMR names appear in any of the six picked clips** (Zad - 3, 4, 5, 13, 15, 17, plus Wahid and Hooman) — checked line by line against the constraint.
