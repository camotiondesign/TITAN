## The idea in one paragraph

A single archive photograph of Tariq at Pharmacy Show 2025, carrying his own line — "Dispensing alone is no longer viable" — with attribution and date, and a question underneath it: a year later, was he right? This is not the answer. It is the tease that announces a strand: Titan is going to go back and check whether its own founder's claim held up. The job of this post is narrow — open curiosity, signal that Titan holds itself to account, and tell the reader (explicitly or implicitly) that the full researched answer is coming later in the runway. It must not pre-empt or thin out the Week 3 anchor carousel that actually does the verdict.
