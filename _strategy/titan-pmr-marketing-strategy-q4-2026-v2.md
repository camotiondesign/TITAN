## Summary

Two years of Titan PMR LinkedIn content generated 363 posts across 20 months. The craft has demonstrably improved — from feature-list brochure copy in early 2025 to observation-first customer-led storytelling by mid-2026. The absolute numbers went in the other direction: per-post reach is down ~33% year-on-year, and total engagement has stayed roughly flat despite 25% more posts shipped.

**Two things we cannot currently tell apart, and both are strategically load-bearing:**
- Whether the reach decline is Titan's content problem, or LinkedIn's algorithm shift affecting most B2B pages in 2026
- Whether the content that shipped has done any commercial job at all (no downstream conversion signal captured)

Q4 does five things:
1. **Doubles down on what's demonstrably worked** (named-operator case studies with hard before/after numbers, event saturation coverage, advocacy-with-action, multi-image posts anchored on a substantive moment)
2. **Executes a targeted investigation into the DOCUMENT format question** (some documents work; some don't; a blanket kill would be wrong — see §2.4)
3. **Instruments the four measurement blind spots** so Q1 2027 decisions can be made on data, not vibes
4. **Runs a small number of controlled tests** on volume, founder-led distribution, and video retention — each with a yes/no answer at Q4 close
5. **Launches podcast as the first owned channel** (separate one-pager, seeded Sep, launches Nov)

## Section 1 — Analysis (honest read of the data)

### 1.1 Year-on-year performance (outliers stripped)

| Metric | 2025 (full) | 2026 (Jan-Jul) | Direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Median impressions per post | 1,321 | 854 | ↓ 35% |
| Mean impressions per post | 1,667 | 1,114 | ↓ 33% |
| Median real engagement rate | 5.39% | 6.87% | ↑ 27% |
| Total reactions | 4,337 | 2,814 | roughly flat monthly |
| Post volume per month | ~16 | ~20 | ↑ 25% |

Per-post reach down, engagement rate up. Higher post volume in 2026 did not compound total engagement.

**Two candidate causes for the 2026 reach decline, and we can't cleanly separate them yet:**
- **Cause A:** LinkedIn platform-wide algorithmic shift in 2026 (well-documented across B2B pages)
- **Cause B:** Titan's own volume increase (~25%) suppressing per-post reach

Both are plausible. Section 4 proposes the LinkedIn API integration + Tariq-led B2B benchmarking work that would resolve this by Q4 close. For now the strategy assumes it's a mix and doesn't over-commit either way.

### 1.2 What worked (data-backed)

- **Named-operator case studies with concrete before/after numbers.** Peak posts: Prabjaudt (Priory Pharmacy, 6,727 impressions, 12.49% real ER), Yusuf ("170,000 items a month sounds impressive, living it is something else" — 3,807 imp, 11.48%), Akbar (2,942 imp, 10.91%). These outperform every other format on both reach and engagement.
- **Multi-image posts anchored on a substantive moment.** The 1,000th pharmacy post, Titanverse Early Adopter Day, Pharmacy Show wrap-up, Medichem visit — all multi-image (Metricool mislabelled some as TEXT — see §1.5). Averaged 23% real engagement rate across the 12 examples. Best single format on the page.
- **Event saturation coverage.** Pharmacy Show 2025 (Oct) and TitanUp 26 (Jun) both delivered the two best sustained content windows on record. Named-people wrap-ups (Post 164, Post 320) consistently outperformed.
- **Advocacy with an action attached.** Highest share counts on record. "We were asked to build this functionality. We refused." (14 shares). Petition posts on funding and IP earned the most re-distribution.
- **Cultural/community posts** (Eid, Ramadan, Diwali, Technician Day) achieved the highest engagement rates on the page. Small reach, disproportionate warmth. Worth shipping deliberately, worth not building strategy on.

### 1.3 What didn't work (data-backed)

- **Fourth-plus cuts on any case study.** Sagar's arc decayed from 5,356 impressions to under 1,000 across six cuts. Intra-story decay is the single clearest pattern in the dataset.
- **Feature-listicle posts with no named human.** The modal early-2025 format. Never a top performer.
- **Blog-teaser reposts** of the same URL four to five times per month. Diminishing returns each cycle.
- **Case-study-as-DOCUMENT** where the story was better as VIDEO or IMAGE (see §2.4).
- **Unmeasured video volume.** 44% of all output is video. Retention was measured on 31 posts (Mar-Nov 2025), average watch time 12 seconds. 75 videos since Feb 2026 have zero retention data. Largest single production commitment on the page, running unmeasured.

### 1.4 What we thought didn't work but shouldn't blanket-kill

- **DOCUMENT format** appeared underperforming when measured by platform "engagement" (click-inflated). Investigation now shows the picture is more mixed — some documents genuinely worked, others didn't. See §2.4 for the workstream. This section is a correction on the previous read.
- **TEXT posts** appeared to underperform in the first read. Cross-checked against per-post `metrics.json`: Titan hasn't shipped any pure text-only posts. What Metricool called "TEXT" was actually multi-image posts where the caption is the primary content. Data classification issue, not a content issue.

### 1.5 Measurement gaps (five blind spots)

1. **Follower count over time.** Without this, we cannot separate audience stagnation from algorithmic throttling. Two entirely different diagnoses, both plausible.
2. **Paid/boost flags per post.** Post 103 (72,479 impressions, 21 reactions) and Post 214 (13,615 impressions, 0.12%) show the signature of paid boosts. All averaged reach numbers are polluted by these.
3. **Video retention on the 75 videos since Feb 2026.** Above.
4. **Downstream conversion signal.** Zero linkage between content shipped and demo bookings, sign-ups, or contract closes. Without this, "did any of this sell software" — the question the budget hangs on — cannot be answered.
5. **Metricool post type classification is unreliable.** Cross-checking against Titan's own per-post `metrics.json` shows Metricool mislabels multi-image posts as TEXT (12 instances found). Any future analytics work must cross-check the type field against the ground truth.

### 1.6 Website traffic reality check

Both blog-teaser posts and case-study posts on LinkedIn are demonstrably not driving meaningful traffic to titanpmr.com (per Google Analytics). Historical assumption that this content built the funnel is not supported. The blog-machine era of 2025 was efficient content generation with a weaker-than-assumed commercial payoff. Q4 does not double down on that pattern.

## Section 2 — Strategy (Q4 2026)

### 2.1 The Q4 thesis in one line

**Ship fewer posts, ship stronger posts, instrument what we ship, resolve the algorithm-vs-content question by Q4 close, and build one owned distribution channel (podcast) that doesn't depend on LinkedIn's reach algorithm.**

### 2.2 Pillars to double down on

1. **Named-operator case study engine.** Butt Lane (Rahul, Stoke), Road Trip (Glevum / Parklands / Morrill), and continuing customer visits. Max 3 cuts per story, spaced over a month, never two siblings within four days. Delivered as multi-image or short-form video, rarely as DOCUMENT.
2. **Hooman-led thought-leadership drumbeat.** The Hub Question campaign already scheduled Aug 4 → Sep 9 (11 posts). Extends into September with Hooman COO Demos taking over as weekly rhythm from Sep 1.
3. **Event coverage saturation.** Pharmacy Show 2026 (October). Coverage plan booked now. Two best sustained windows on the calendar in 20 months both came from event saturation (Pharmacy Show '25, TitanUp '26).
4. **Advocacy-with-action.** Currently under-used (two posts in 20 months, both top-shared). One per month minimum for Q4.
5. **Multi-image anchored on substantive moments.** Milestones, event wraps, site visits with named people. Best-performing format on the page by real engagement rate.

### 2.3 Kill list (already executed today, 3 Aug)

32 posts moved to Archive:
- All Tariq leftover Idea/In Production posts (19)
- All Rahul Puri leftover keynote content (6)
- Weak TitanUp scatter quotes (5)
- Past-dated ProPharmace fillers (1)
- Wahid AutomationVsHub (no shoot happened) (1)

**Ongoing kill rules for Q4:**
- No repeat blog-teaser of the same URL within a month
- No fourth cut on any case-study arc
- No feature-listicle without a named human
- No generic "Titan is amazing" copy — replace with Idea-First Editorial framework (customer as proof, not premise)

### 2.4 DOCUMENT format — targeted investigation (Q4 workstream)

A blanket kill on documents would be wrong. Preliminary ranking by non-click engagement (impressions + likes + reposts, clicks stripped):

**Top documents (per 1000 impressions):**
- Advocacy with an action attached ("Bulk nomination is undermining patient choice") — 30.7
- Cultural/warmth posts (Eid, Ramadan)
- Sector-authority commentary ("Right now there's a huge conversation happening about AI in pharmacy") — 45.8
- Educational frameworks ("If you want your pharmacy to grow, you can't rely on old systems") — 38.0

**Bottom documents:**
- Case-study-as-DOCUMENT (Yusuf, Asif, Prabjaudt swipe-through carousels) — case studies work better as video or single image, not as swipeable documents
- Feature-explainer without a strong hook (Mounjaro prices, NHS 10-year plan carousel)

**Pattern emerging:** documents that make an ARGUMENT (advocacy, authority commentary, educational framework) work. Documents that tell a STORY (case study as swipe-through) don't — because people swipe, don't engage.

**Q4 workstream:**
- Cross-reference each document post against on-slide content (in the post's per-slide caption / JSON metadata)
- Cluster by document intent (argument / story / feature-list / educational / advocacy)
- Identify which intents work
- Issue "when to use DOCUMENT" rule by end of Sep for use in Oct + Q1 planning

### 2.5 Controlled tests (each with success criteria)

Three tests, each with a clear yes/no answer at Q4 close:

1. **Founder-led distribution test.** Hooman personal profile reposts one hero post per week from Sep 1. **Success:** ≥30% lift in impressions on those posts vs page-only baseline over 8 weeks. Tariq to lead the parallel B2B benchmarking research (§2.6).
2. **Volume rollback test.** Cut LinkedIn cadence to 3-4 posts/week (from ~5) outside event windows through Sep. **Success:** ≥20% lift in per-post reach on remaining posts. **Failure or flat result = the reach decline was algorithmic, not volume-caused, which reshapes Q1 planning.**
3. **Video retention re-instrumentation.** Every video shipped Q4 has watch time captured. **Success:** knowing by Q1 whether video at 44% output is earning its production cost. If avg watch time is still 12 seconds, video budget shifts to first-3-second discipline or lower share of output.

### 2.6 Tariq-led B2B benchmarking (new workstream)

Tariq's ask: look at other B2B firms in similar spots — SaaS, health tech, professional services — and identify:
- Which are seeing similar 2026 LinkedIn reach declines (is this an industry pattern, or Titan-specific?)
- Which have found workarounds (founder-led profiles, paid, community, podcasts, newsletters)
- What is measurably working in B2B distribution right now

Output: one-page brief by end of Sep to inform Q1 2027 plan.

### 2.7 Podcast launch

See separate one-pager. Launches Nov 2026 as new pillar. Studio build-out Sep. Recording begins Oct. First 4 episodes bank before public launch.

## Section 3 — Monthly content template (per month, not week-by-week)

Wahid asked for the template, not the daily schedule. Each month should contain roughly:

| Content type | Target volume / month | Format |
|---|---|---|
| Named-operator case study (hero + max 2 cuts) | 2-3 arcs (~6-9 posts) | Video primary, multi-image secondary |
| Hooman-led thought-leadership | 4 posts | Video, carousel, quote card mix |
| Educational / buyer-enablement | 2 posts | Carousel or short video |
| Advocacy-with-action | 1 post | Whatever fits the moment |
| Event or partner spotlight | 2-3 posts (heavier during event weeks) | Multi-image or video |
| Cultural / community | 1-2 posts (calendar-driven) | Single image or short video |
| Reactive / news commentary | 1-2 posts | Quote card or carousel |

**Target total: 16-22 posts/month** — lower than current 20-24/month, aligned with the volume rollback test.

**Event windows (Pharmacy Show October) override this template** — go heavy for the fortnight.

**Content sourcing responsibility:**
- Case studies + event coverage + partner spotlights: Cam-led (site visits, interviews, editing)
- Hooman thought-leadership: Hooman recording, Cam editing + captioning
- Educational + buyer-enablement carousels: needs a Titan-side subject-matter expert to seed the argument, Cam to shape and design (Cam won't write these solo — they need insider content authority)
- Advocacy: policy-triggered, Cam-led with Wahid sign-off for anything sector-sensitive
- Cultural: Cam-led

## Section 4 — Workflow + Infrastructure Ask

### 4.1 What's working operationally

- Notion editorial board — full 220+ post pipeline, integrated with Notion Social for direct scheduling
- Studio OS content intake — Nas and other on-site staff upload directly, AI pipeline (Whisper + Claude) transcribes + analyses batches. Discussed with Wahid Aug 4 as the platform-instead-of-crew approach.
- AI-augmented editorial (ChatGPT + Claude) for captions, campaign planning, seeded from customer transcripts and interview archives
- Design system in Claude Design — brand-native image generation, Titan blue palette + Newake typography
- Metrics via Metricool for platform data + per-post `metrics.json` archived per campaign

### 4.2 Bottlenecks

1. **Video edit throughput.** Biggest current bottleneck. Hooman COO Demos backlog is proof — 25+ days In Progress with zero time logged. Road Trip 7-post campaign needs 3-4 video edits + one 90-sec montage. Podcast adds ~4 hours edit time per episode when it starts.
2. **Post-shoot content triage.** Batches land in Studio OS. AI analysis proposes campaigns. Cam then re-cuts captions + folder-organises assets. That last mile is manual and slow.
3. **Photo folderisation.** 411 Marton Road photos still unsorted. Similar volume on Road Trip. Weeks of low-value manual work.
4. **Podcast post-production capability.** Not yet in workflow at all.
5. **Cross-brand + cross-campaign coordination.** Titan PMR ↔ Titanverse ↔ TitanUp all touch each other. Currently held together by Cam alone.

### 4.3 Resource ask — reframed

**Not "editor" — creative producer.**

The gap isn't someone who can chop faster. The gap is someone who can help Cam think strategically about priorities, run the second-brain on campaign shape, and take execution off Cam's plate so the strategic role Wahid asked for can actually happen. Second-brain is more valuable than second-editor.

**Short-term (Sep-Oct):**
- **1 x creative producer**, part-time or contractor basis, ~2-3 days/week. Someone who can:
  - Take the campaign brief from Cam + own the post-production pipeline (Hooman COO Demos, Road Trip cutdowns, event coverage, eventually podcast)
  - Run the day-of on shoot days (Cam directs, they capture and organise)
  - Own the content triage last-mile from Studio OS intake
  - Same person also owns photo triage
- Highest single ROI resource this quarter. Right hire lets Cam step into the strategic slot Wahid asked for; wrong hire (pure edit hands) doesn't.

**Medium-term (Q1 2027):**
- If podcast lands + needs weekly rhythm → permanent audio/video editor
- If Q4 test 2 (volume rollback) reveals reach is algorithm-not-volume, additional resource shifts from more-posts to better-posts

### 4.4 Infrastructure — LinkedIn API integration

Currently reliant on Metricool for LinkedIn data. Two problems:
- Metricool exports mislabel post types (see §1.5)
- We're paying a middleware to fetch data we can pull directly from LinkedIn
- Metricool can't backfill historical data or provide follower-count snapshots on the cadence we need

**Proposal:** integrate LinkedIn's Marketing Developer API directly. Feed data into Studio OS. Own the pipeline. Estimated dev time: 1-2 weeks Claude Code session. Benefits:
- Accurate post type classification
- Automated daily/weekly snapshots including follower count
- Enables the boost-flag capture that fixes measurement gap #2
- Removes Metricool subscription cost long-term

### 4.5 Blog reality

The 2025 blog-machine era demonstrably did not drive titanpmr.com traffic. Q4 does not repeat that pattern.

If Titan does want blogs going forward, they must come from Titan insiders with genuine subject-matter authority (Wahid on AI-checking layer, Hooman on hub economics, Sajid on sales operations, Tariq on sector strategy). Cam-written blogs based on secondary research are worth less than the effort.

## Section 5 — What we'll measure

Quarterly cycle only works if we measure. Four numbers, monthly:

1. **Total impressions per platform** (LinkedIn + IG + FB + podcast downloads once live)
2. **Median real engagement rate** (clicks stripped for DOCUMENT / MULTIIMAGE formats)
3. **Follower count** — snapshot first of every month, added to `analytics/monthly_snapshot.json` (blocked until LinkedIn API integration ships)
4. **Boost/paid flag** captured at publish, kept out of averages

Plus one that requires Titan team involvement:

5. **Demo booking count per week, tagged to what content shipped that week.** The "did any of this sell software" question. Wahid help needed to establish this loop with the sales team.
