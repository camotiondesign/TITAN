# Trustpilot Single-Image Post — Oadby Village Review

**For:** Claude design agent
**Format:** Single image, 4:5 (1080 × 1350) — LinkedIn feed native

## Purpose

Stat-card-style social post celebrating a verified Trustpilot review of Titan PMR that specifically credits the Titan implementation team for making a robot-integration go-live smooth. Fits the road-trip / shared-purpose campaign that pairs Titan with robotic-partner content — this is the review-side proof that Titan's team handles the robot-integration workflow well from the customer's own words.

## What goes on the image

### Kicker (top)
5-STAR REVIEW · TRUSTPILOT

### Hero verbatim quote (main visual weight)
*"Nas and Maj were great to work with. Really helped us and the team get through the initial days especially with robot integration and our collection point."*

Quote must be in quote marks. Verbatim as written. Do not edit.

### Attribution block (bottom, in a Trustpilot-style card)
- **Reviewer:** Oadby Village
- **Verified Trustpilot review** (no date — Cam's rule: date is dropped to keep older reviews feeling contemporary; provenance still carried by "Verified Trustpilot review" + 5-star row + reviewer name)
- **5 green Trustpilot stars** row

### Trustpilot logo
Trustpilot green star + wordmark, footer or attribution card. For provenance.

### Titan brand mark
TITAN wordmark, opposite corner to Trustpilot. Standard placement per brand system.

## What NOT to include

- No sales CTA ("Book a demo", "Learn more") — this is warmth, not conversion
- No hashtags on the image (they live in the caption)
- No competitor names (there are none in the source, but noting for the editorial gate)
- No em dashes anywhere in typography
- No emoji hook substitutes
