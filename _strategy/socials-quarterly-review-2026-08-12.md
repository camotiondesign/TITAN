## Section 3. Campaign sequence retrospective

The `campaigns/` directory is empty, so campaigns were rebuilt by clustering post dates and captions in the exports.

### Pharmacy Show 2025 (autumn 2025)
Sequence: 20 posts across 3 Jul → 18 Nov, but the dense window is 7 Oct → 27 Oct (12 posts). Total: 38,115 impressions, ~5,320 engagements.
- Buildup: countdown singles (5 days, 3 days, 2 days) at 542-922 imp each. Low individual but they compound.
- Peak: day-one video 2,900, album 3,185, Tariq keynote 4,632, Zad interviews 2,298, Zainab Pillsorted 2,535.
- Afterglow: BMP long-form 3,807 on 18 Nov (case study explicitly framed as "we met them at the show").
- What worked: daily cadence during show week, mixing wide-angle wrap videos with named-owner talking heads. Every peak day had a face and a name attached.
- What did not: the "5 days / 3 days / 2 days" countdown template repeated verbatim between Titan and Titanverse pages on the same days. Cannibalisation, not stacking.

### TitanUp 26 (Feb reveal → June event)
Sequence: 53 posts across 17 Feb → 6 Jul. Total: 51,832 impressions, ~14,162 engagements.
- Reveal: 17 Feb recce (1,613 imp) → 26 Feb QR teaser (1,910) → 2 Mar hard launch. Reveal earned 5,760 imp across three posts on 2 Mar.
- Drumbeat: sponsor and speaker announces through March, April, May. Individual posts stayed in the 400-900 band; the drumbeat volume kept it in feed but did not compound.
- Peak: 7 Jun-9 Jun. The event days delivered the single biggest post of the year to date, "just getting started and it's buzzing already" (4,013 imp, 132.7% ER, multi-image). Six posts across three days pulled 15,000+ imp.
- Afterglow: "a week on from TitanUp 26" multi-image (14 Jun, 2,225 imp @ 61.7% ER). Then it faded.
- What worked: multi-image field-of-event posts. Every one of them punched over 1,700.
- What did not: the "3 posts of the same content in different formats on 2 Mar" strategy (hard-launch carousel, TitanUp reveal carousel, TitanUp 2026 document). Combined they returned ~3,848 imp, which is what a single strong carousel would have done alone. The document format did not add reach.

### 1000th pharmacy milestone (July 2025)
Sequence: 2 posts on 11 Jul (4,785 imp) and 15 Jul (montage video 1,803 imp). Total: 6,588 imp.
- What worked: milestone framing on a single image with the number front and centre.
- What did not: only 2 posts. A milestone this big deserved a sequence: leader quotes, customer reactions, behind-the-scenes of the 1000th install. The wave was left on the table.

### Mounjaro pricing (Aug 2025 → recurring)
Sequence: one carousel on 27 Aug 2025 (3,422 imp, ER 104.1%). Adjacent posts on 31 Jul (Sagar 4x growth) and 13 Aug (Puri health hub) leaned into the same trend. Total for the three: 7,507 imp.
- What worked: a carousel on a pricing story that owners were live-negotiating.
- What did not: no follow-up when prices moved again in Q4 2025 or in 2026. This is a beat we own and we are not touching it. Cf. rec #3.

### £158m clawback (Jan 2026)
Sequence: single carousel on 29 Jan 2026 (1,507 imp).
- What worked: nothing above baseline. The topic is huge, the post was small.
- What did not: single-shot policy takes have consistently underperformed at Titan. Policy needs a sequence with owner reactions to compound. This was a miss.

### The Titan Way film (March-May 2025)

### ProPharmace Future Pharmacist Conference (July 2026)

- What did not: the buildup (2 Jul, 684 imp) and the day-of (8 Jul, 344 and 314 imp) undersold the moment. This event has bigger legs than we gave it.

### Titanverse launch (Oct-Nov 2025)
Sequence: 14 posts, 7 Oct → 12 Nov 2025. Total: 14,816 imp.
- Countdown: 5 days (1,075), first webinar (1,073), 3 days loop video (2,119, highest of the launch), 2 days (599).
- Reveal: "what is Titanverse" video (1,017), one app one flow (794), how much is in Titanverse (881), time for services (2,019).
- Afterglow: chaos to clarity (1,196), AI agents ask anything (1,135), POV consultation (908 with 76.8% ER), everything app (1,013).
- What worked: the countdown had a strong Day 3 hit; the "time for services" positioning line (2,019) validated the language we are now building the manifesto on.
- What did not: the launch peaked and then the page never sustained. From 10 posts a month in Q4 2025, TV has slid to 1 post in July 2026 and nothing since 2 Jul.

### Patterns that repeatedly pay off

1. **Multi-image field visits.** Every multi-image post above 1,500 imp in 2026 is a field visit or an event floor set. It is the single most reliable format we have.
2. **Named-owner long-form video.** Prabjaudt, Yusuf, Sagar, Rahul. Six of the top ten Titan videos ever are named-owner talking heads. Anonymous videos average roughly half the reach.
3. **Contrarian POV carousels.** Mounjaro pricing (3,422), Pharmacy First walk-away (3,195), IP fails (3,522), pharmacy isn't struggling (2,525). Argument-shaped carousels reliably clear 2,500 imp.
4. **Curiosity hook on a familiar object.** "Can you spot the difference?" (7,338), "Look familiar?" (5,747), "Two labels." pattern works because the reveal is inside the image, not the caption.
5. **Daily cadence during event weeks.** Pharmacy Show 2025 and TitanUp 26 both used this. When we drop 3-5 posts across event days with mixed formats, the compounded reach outperforms the same posts spread across three weeks.
6. **Milestone-and-immediate-followup.** The Buzzing multi-image on TitanUp Day 1 hit 4,013. It worked because it landed in the first hour of the event and used real, immediate proof. Milestones dropped 24 hours late lose most of their power.
7. **Warm-tone human moment, no product mention.** "Yesterday's inaugural Future Pharmacist Conference was fire" (160% ER) and "The best sign a training session has landed" (1,784 imp). Removing the plug increases the reach.

### Patterns to kill

1. **Three formats of the same content on the same day.** 2 Mar 2026 TitanUp reveal. 2 Feb Head Office Day had a hint of this too. It confuses the feed and cannibalises reach.
2. **Countdown singles that just count down.** 5 days / 3 days / 2 days image posts underperform the countdowns that added a substantive line ("3 days loop" video on Titanverse hit 2,119 because it also showed the product).
3. **Single-shot policy takes.** Clawback (1,507 alone), any solo carousel on a national trend. If the topic is big enough to post about, it is big enough for three posts.
4. **Hero film as a whole content plan.** Titan Way film became a 10-post reheat cycle. Films need a launch beat and then the campaign moves on.
5. **Testimonials in static single-image cards.** The Oadby 11 Aug post (260 imp) is the latest example. Multi-image field visits carry the same message to 4-5x the audience.

## Section 4. Where socials are right now

The last four weeks on the Titan page: 4,767 imp (14 Jul-20 Jul), 8,070 imp (21 Jul-27 Jul), 4,828 imp (28 Jul-3 Aug), 3,133 imp (4 Aug-11 Aug). The trend is down and the most recent week is the weakest since early June. Total impressions per post has drifted from ~1,600 to ~630 in a fortnight.

The mechanism is clear from the format analysis. Multi-image share dropped 19 points month-on-month and total impressions dropped 17.7%. Video volume held but only a third of videos cleared 1,500 imp. Single-image is doing more of the work and returning less per unit.

Titanverse is the bigger risk flag. Zero posts in the repo since 2 Jul, down from a monthly cadence of 8-11 posts through Q4 2025 and Q1 2026. The page is not underperforming per post (801 imp/post 2026 YTD, 9.0% ER), it is not shipping. The manifesto on 19 Aug is the correct restart, but it needs a companion drumbeat, not a single carousel every fortnight, or the momentum will not build.

Instagram matched 13 posts, top view 731 (Google-search analogy Reel). Facebook matched 3 posts, top view 109. Neither platform is generating attention worth reallocating effort to right now; they should stay as republish channels until we decide otherwise.

Momentum flags: multi-image field visits still hit hard (Marton Road 1,765 at 26.6% ER, Community Pharmacy Herts 1,124). The Boots-as-foil hook on 31 Jul (1,825 at 11.3% ER) is the first proof the Idea-First editorial voice pulls when we point it at a competitor rather than at ourselves.

**Single biggest thing in the next two weeks:** ship three multi-image field posts before 26 Aug, and use the manifesto carousel on 19 Aug to open a five-post Titanverse burst, not a single drop. Fill the empty 13-17 Aug calendar slots with Trustpilot-driven repurposes (rec #1 or rec #10). If we do only one of these, do the multi-image drum. It is the only format still compounding.
