## PART 1 — Who this is for

Same audience as Doc 1. **UK community pharmacy owners.** But the podcast serves a specific slice of what they want that no other Titan content can serve:

**They want to hear other pharmacy owners talk honestly about the job for an hour.** In their own words. About the P&L, the team, the mistakes, the wins. Nobody else in the sector is offering that.

**They will consume it:**
- On YouTube, evenings/weekends, sat on the sofa with a phone or a TV
- On Spotify/Apple in the car between sites
- In clip form on LinkedIn during 10-minute morning scrolls
- On WhatsApp when another owner sends them "have you watched this?"

**The podcast doesn't compete with Titan's LinkedIn content — it compounds it.** LinkedIn case studies are 3-minute proofs of a specific transformation. Podcast episodes are 45-60 minute conversations about how someone actually runs their business. Same audience, different depth, different consumption moment.

## PART 2 — Why podcast, why now (data-backed)

**1. LinkedIn reach is throttling.** Industry-wide B2B Company Page reach down ~60% over 2 years (independent research from Richard van der Blom's annual 1.3M-post LinkedIn algorithm study, corroborated by Metricool's 673K-post analysis). LinkedIn moved 65% of feed real estate to personal profiles, 5% to Company Pages. Owned distribution matters more than ever.

**2. YouTube compounds.** Our own data: **53.4% of all YouTube views come from videos 30+ days old.** First video (Jun 2025) still pulling 40-60 views/week 13 months later. Podcast episodes compound the same way — LinkedIn posts don't.

**3. The "owner-operator" podcast lane is empty.** Full teardown in `/Downloads/pharmacy-planet-teardown.md`. Pharmacy Planet Podcast is *about* the sector (contracts, funding, group operators). Nobody is doing *by/for* individual owners with the "how do you actually run this?" frame.

**4. Titan has a moat that competitors don't.** ChatGPT commoditised feature comparisons. What isn't commoditised is real domain experts on camera talking honestly. Wahid on integration, Hooman on economics, Tariq on sector, Jeff on data, Sajid on growth. This is what a personality-forward B2B moat looks like in 2026.

**5. The hosting infrastructure is already built.** New office basement studio. Multi-cam, controlled lighting. No travel for hosts. One hour of recording = 1 episode + 6-10 short clips + quote cards + LinkedIn posts + newsletter fodder + YouTube longform. Most efficient content unit in the mix.

## PART 3 — The competitive picture (Pharmacy Planet + how we beat them)

**Pharmacy Planet is beatable on LinkedIn inside two months. The real fight is on YouTube.**

**Their position:**
- 522 LinkedIn followers, 200,000 YouTube subs (doubled from 100K in ~3 months)
- YouTube-first. LinkedIn is a satellite.
- Hosted by Boo Dhaliwal, in-studio wood-slat set, red sofa, condenser mics
- Guest list includes Paul Mayberry (PharmacyX — our direct competitor)
- Ships 1 post per episode. In-studio only. No BTS, no shop-floor content.

**Their weaknesses (the doors they leave open):**
1. **They don't use LinkedIn Collaborations.** Zero collab posts in the whole scroll. Their best-performing post is a guest's *repost*.
2. **Captions bury the lede** with producer credits ("In this clip from The Pharmacy Planet Podcast, [Guest] and [Host]…")
3. **Hashtag maximalism** (15+ per post)
4. **Muddled dual audience** (B2B pharmacy owners + B2C wellness cross-pollinating badly)
5. **Only 1 post per episode** — no atomisation into quote cards / carousels / written insights
6. **Studio-only visuals** — no in-pharmacy shots for cold-opens

**How we beat them on LinkedIn:**
- Every episode ships as a **guest Collab post** (they don't) — double distribution across our 6K + their 500-2K follower audiences
- **Quote-in-headline captions** (TED-style — see Part 6) — they bury the lede
- **4 post types per episode** (they ship 1) — clip + carousel + written insight + collab thumbnail
- **Shoot some episodes IN the guest's pharmacy** — closer to Diary of a CEO visual language than a wood-slat studio

**How we survive the YouTube fight:** we don't out-scale them by December. We warm YouTube through Q3-Q4 with case study longforms (target: 1,500 monthly views by end of October, up from 500-600). Podcast lands into a channel already gaining momentum, not a cold start. Long game.

**Don't underestimate:** if we launch, expect Pharmacy Planet to try booking Titan customers as guests, and expect PharmacyX to lean on Pharmacy Planet as their podcast channel of choice. **Position early. Book the best owners before PharmacyX does.**

## PART 4 — Format

**Working name:** *Diary of a Pharmacy Owner*
(Alternatives: *The Pharmacy Owner Diaries* · *Running a Pharmacy* · *The Titan Diaries* — Wahid pick)

**Concept:** genuine conversation with a UK independent pharmacy owner about the business of running a pharmacy today. Not a Titan product show. Not a features rundown. A "how do you actually do this" show that owners send each other.

**Style reference:** *Diary of a CEO* (Steven Bartlett). Long-form conversation, professional set, warm register, guest-first questions. Owner talking honestly with a peer.

**Length:** 45-60 min episodes. Cut into 4-6 short vertical clips per episode.

**Cadence:** Weekly, dropping every Tuesday. Bank 4 episodes before Nov launch.

**Recording:** In-studio at new office basement, dedicated set. Guest travels to Titan HQ (or we go to them for select episodes — shot in their own pharmacy for visual differentiation). Filmed 4K, multi-cam.

### Host rotation (Titan C-suite)

Not every host on every episode. Matched to guest's strongest topic.

| Host | Best for guests who want to talk about... |
|---|---|
| **Wahid Muhammad (CTO)** | Technology roadmap, AI, integration philosophy |
| **Tariq Muhammad (CEO)** | Sector strategy, movement-level narrative |
| **Hooman Ghalamkari (COO)** | Business economics, service pivot, hub-and-spoke |
| **Jeff Shelley (CIO)** | Data, metrics, what pharmacies should measure |
| **Sajid Ramzan (CSO)** | Growth stories, multi-site scale |

Rotating hosts is **structural differentiation Pharmacy Planet can't copy** — they're built around one host.

### Episode structure (60 min target)

1. **Cold open (0-3 min):** guest tells one specific story from their week
2. **How you got here (3-15 min):** origin story
3. **The real job today (15-35 min):** how the business actually runs
4. **Where you're going (35-50 min):** the plan
5. **What most people get wrong (50-58 min):** the advice section — strongest quotes come from here
6. **Closer (58-60 min):** one thing they wish the industry was talking about more

## PART 5 — The distribution mechanic (the whole game)

**This is where we beat Pharmacy Planet. It's mechanical, it's replicable, it's the reason to ship.**

### Every episode ships as 4 post types on LinkedIn

Not 1 (like Pharmacy Planet). **4.**

| Post | Format | When |
|---|---|---|
| 1. **The Collab clip** | 9:16 vertical, 2-4 min TED-style, LinkedIn Collaboration with the guest | Day of episode release (Tuesday) |
| 2. **The quote card** | Single-image pull-quote | +3 days |
| 3. **The carousel summary** | Document / carousel — 5-8 slides of key insights | +5 days |
| 4. **The written insight** | Long-form text post, host's personal profile + Titan page | +7 days |

Plus rolling in parallel:
- 3-6 additional short clips across TikTok / YouTube Shorts / Instagram Reels
- 1 full episode on YouTube (the mothership asset, uploaded before LinkedIn Collab clip)
- Newsletter feature (if launching alongside)
- Podcast landing page slot on titanpmr.com/podcast

### LinkedIn Collaborations — the mechanic Pharmacy Planet missed

When we post as a LinkedIn Collaboration with the guest, the post appears in **both** Titan's feed (6K followers) AND the guest's feed (typically 500-2,000 followers). Double distribution. No algorithm penalty. Guest doesn't have to write anything if we author the caption.

**At Cam's scale (team of one), this isn't just smart — it's structurally necessary.** DoaC hires three specialists for social. We use LinkedIn's native mechanic to get equivalent leverage.

### YouTube is where the podcast actually lives

Full episode goes to YouTube first. LinkedIn Collab clip funnels traffic there. Podcast lives on Spotify + Apple Podcasts. **YouTube is the mothership.** Every LinkedIn post has a soft-footer YouTube link (never a hard "click here" push — Amanda Natividad zero-click principle).

## PART 6 — TED-style caption template (the actual template)

**Steal directly from TED** (full teardown in `/Desktop/TED_LinkedIn_Teardown.md`). Their caption pattern hits 1,000+ reactions per post consistently. We adapt it for pharmacy owners.

### The template

```
"[Verbatim pull-quote from the guest — the strongest single line from the episode]"

[One line of observation reframing what the quote proves. Idea-First: what changes about what deserves a pharmacist's attention?]

Learn how [Guest Name] [specific transformation]: [t.titanpmr.com/{shortlink}]
```

### Worked examples

**Prabjaudt Bakhshi episode:**
> *"If it wasn't for Titan, I'd have left pharmacy a long time ago."*
>
> Five years ago Prabjaudt was ready to walk. Now his pharmacy dispenses 16,000 items a month with no overtime and no burnout.
>
> Learn how Prabjaudt rebuilt Priory Pharmacy: t.titanpmr.com/prabjaudt

**Rahul (Butt Lane) episode:**
> *"Every ACT in my pharmacy is now clinical. Every one of them."*
>
> When your accuracy checking technicians stop being a bottleneck and start being a clinical team, the whole business changes.
>
> Learn how Rahul turned Butt Lane into a clinical services engine: t.titanpmr.com/rahul

### Rules (British English, Titan voice)

- **No em dashes** (competitor tell — PharmacyX uses these everywhere)
- **No "proud/delighted/excited to announce"** (competitor tell)
- **No hashtags over 3** (Pharmacy Planet uses 15+ — reads amateur)
- **No emoji hook substitutes** (🚀 🤔 ✅)
- **No "curious what you think" bait**
- **No default closing 💙** (own rule — earn emoji, don't default)
- **Named attribution always** — first name + last name + pharmacy + location
- **Soft footer link only** — never "Link in comments 👇"

**The clip does the hooking. The caption does the framing. The link is footer.**

## PART 7 — Clip anatomy (visual specification)

Based on TED's on-screen system + Pharmacy Planet's studio pipeline + Diary of a CEO's celebrity-doc treatment.

- **9:16 vertical**, letterboxed inside LinkedIn's post frame
- **Named-operator anchor card top-left** — Titan blue rectangle, 2 lines: "[FIRST NAME] [SURNAME] / OWNER, [PHARMACY], [CITY]"
- **Titan brand watermark top-right** in white circle
- **Burned-in subtitles bottom-centre**, bold consistent typeface (never LinkedIn autocaptions)
- **Cold open on the guest's punchiest line**. No intro card, no logo sting, no "hello and welcome". Frame one = guest mid-sentence
- **Keep the studio set visible** — controlled lighting, backdrop, mic (part of the read, don't crop it out)
- **No endcard/CTA in the clip.** CTA lives in the caption.
- **Length: 2-4 minutes** — TED's standard, self-contained argument, NOT a 60-sec teaser (Wistia data: 30+min video only 3% engagement dip vs 10% for 3-5min — long-form has structural staying power)

**Visual signature that carries authority without a TED red circle** (the "where the model breaks for Titan" note from the TED teardown): Titan needs to build its own visual system. Design job for Q3.

**Note — proof of concept already on the page:** three podcast-style clips (Hooman on Boots, Sunil at TitanUp, Rahul Gogna at TitanUp) all performed above baseline. Rahul's 13.23% ER is top 5-10% of Titan posts ever. These prove the format works. They need the anchor card + watermark + longer duration + TED caption template to become the podcast spec proper.
