## PART 1 — Who this is for

**UK community pharmacy owners.** Roughly 11,500 across England, Scotland, Wales, Northern Ireland. Independent operators, multi-site groups, family businesses. They open Sundays. They stayed open in the pandemic. Right now they're being squeezed by margin cuts, hub-and-spoke legislation, funding renegotiation, and hub competition from Boots and Well.

**What they care about, in order:**
1. Cash flow this month
2. Their team not burning out
3. Getting the workflow off paper before it kills them
4. Doing more clinical work without hiring more people
5. What the sector news actually means for their business

**How they consume content:**
- LinkedIn feed: 10-15 minutes a day, mostly on mobile, mostly on the sofa in the evening
- YouTube: longer sessions, evenings and weekends, where they'll actually watch a full 20-minute story
- Sector newsletters: skimmed at breakfast (P3, C+D, CPCS bulletins)
- WhatsApp groups: where the real trade happens (owner-to-owner, invitation only)

**What they do NOT want:** feature demos, "book a demo today", polished vendor marketing. They can smell it in the first line.

**What they DO want:** other pharmacy owners on camera, honestly, telling them how they did the thing they're trying to do. Real numbers. Real mistakes. Real workflow.

**Everything below serves this reader. If a recommendation doesn't earn its place under "does this help a pharmacy owner run their business better", it doesn't ship.**

**Titan LinkedIn baseline (2026):**

- Median 854 impressions per post
- Top-quartile (75th pct): 25 reactions
- Peak on record: 121 reactions (1,000th pharmacy multi-image)

**Titan YouTube baseline:**
- 167 videos, 72,895 total views, 409 watch hours, 150 subscribers
- 5.43% impressions CTR (industry benchmark 4-6%, so above par)
- **53.4% of ALL views come from videos 30+ days old** — the compounding effect measured
- First video ever (Jun 2025) still pulling 40-60 views/week 13 months later

**LinkedIn industry context — why this matters:**

Independent researcher Richard van der Blom publishes the most widely-cited LinkedIn algorithm report each year, analysing 1.3M+ B2B posts. Social analytics vendor Metricool runs a parallel study across 673K posts. Both landed on the same conclusion: **B2B Company Page reach is down ~60% over the last 2 years.** LinkedIn has actively moved feed real estate from Company Pages (down to ~5%) to personal profiles (up to ~65%).

**Titan's YoY drop: ~33%.** Roughly half the industry rate. In an environment where most B2B vendors are losing to the platform, Titan is outperforming it. That's the number that matters when someone asks "is content still worth investing in?" — yes, because we're still gaining share while the sector loses reach.

**Other platform mechanics worth knowing:**
- Posts with external links see ~60% reach cut
- Native video gets 3.5x more reach than YouTube link posts
- Amanda Natividad's "zero-click" principle: the post should be substantial on LinkedIn itself; external links are footer, not the point

**What actually works on Titan's page (peak posts, evidence-based):**
- Named-operator case study heroes (video): Prabjaudt 6,727 imp / 105 reactions; Yusuf 3,807 / 85; Sagar 5,356 / 56

## PART 5 — Case study campaigns (the anchor of every month)

**This is the format that serves the audience best.** A pharmacy owner watches another pharmacy owner tell their story. Nothing in the vendor set touches this format.

### Shoot happens the week BEFORE the month starts

Gives us a week to edit before posting begins on Day 1 of the month.

### YouTube gets the full long-form video FIRST

Not a cutdown — the full 8-15 minute version. YouTube compounds (53.4% of your views come from back-catalogue). LinkedIn's impressions are locked in on day 1. Feed the compounding asset first.

### LinkedIn 5-post sequence — hero at END

Test the reversed order on 1-2 arcs in Q4. Measure YouTube completion rate + LinkedIn hero performance. Keep or revert on evidence.

| Week | Post | Format | Job |
|---|---|---|---|
| Week 1 | Filming visit | Multi-image | Sets up the pharmacy owner as the story. Warmth. |
| Week 2 | Pull-quote card | Single image | Standalone punch. Redistribution. |
| Week 3 | Best insight clip → YouTube | Short-form video, TED-style | First push to YouTube full version. |
| Week 4 | Carousel teasing → YouTube | Document | Deep consumption. Second push. |
| End Week 4 / Start next month | **HERO = full long-form video** | Long-form on LinkedIn (2-4 min cut) | Main event, arrives after 3 weeks of YouTube warmup. |

**Amendment based on the research:** LinkedIn hero is a **90-120 sec highlight remix**, not the raw longform. Protects YouTube session-quality signals (if people watch the same video on LinkedIn instead of YouTube, YouTube's algo reads "this video isn't retaining people" and stops recommending it). YouTube keeps the full version; LinkedIn gets the strongest 2 minutes.

### The clip anatomy (TED-style, evidenced in TED_LinkedIn_Teardown.md)

- 9:16 vertical, 2-4 minutes long — **NOT a 60-sec teaser**. Self-contained argument.
- **Cold open on the pharmacy owner's sharpest line.** Frame one = them mid-sentence with a headline-worthy quote. No "meet Sarah" intro card.
- **Named-operator anchor card top-left** in a Titan blue rectangle: "SARAH CHEN / OWNER, BLOOMSBURY PHARMACY, LONDON"
- **Titan logo top-right** as permanent brand watermark
- **Burned-in subtitles bottom-centre**, bold consistent typeface (never rely on LinkedIn autocaptions)
- **No endcard/CTA in the clip.** CTA lives in the caption.

### The caption template (Idea-First Editorial + TED structure)

```
"[Verbatim pull-quote from the owner in the first 6 seconds of the video]"

[One line of observation about what this proves — Idea-First frame]

[Named attribution: full name, pharmacy, location]

Watch how [Owner] rebuilt [specific thing] to [specific outcome]: [titanpmr.com/stories/{slug} or youtube link]
```

**Rules:** British English, no em dashes, no "proud/delighted to announce", no hashtags over 3, no ✅ green ticks, no 🚀, no "curious what you think" bait, no default closing 💙. The clip does the hooking. The caption does the framing.

### What one shoot day produces
- 5 LinkedIn posts across the month
- 4-8 short TikTok/YouTube Shorts/Instagram Reels clips
- 1 full long-form on YouTube (the mothership asset)
- Bank material for 1 evergreen quote image 3-6 months later (Prabjaudt at 183d = 2,573 imp — highest-leverage low-effort slot on the page)

### Realistic expectations per campaign

| Post | Median imp | Median reactions |
|---|---|---|
| Multi-image visit | 1,500-2,500 | 20-40 |
| Single-image quote | 1,500-3,000 | 25-50 |
| Best insight clip | High variance | — |
| Carousel to YouTube | 2,000-4,000 | 20-35 |
| Hero highlight (90-120s) | 2,500-5,000 | 30-70 |
| YouTube full longform | 200-500 views in month 1, compounding indefinitely | — |

## PART 6 — Micro-campaigns from fresh installs (stocking fillers)

Fresh install content lands via Nas or on-site staff through Studio OS intake. When it comes in, we pump it out same-day or next-day.

**Rules:**
- Fully reliant on implementer providing good context + content — the richer their input, the stronger the post
- **We don't post everything.** Only ones with a real quote, real moment, or strong image
- Format: single image + short caption OR short video clip
- Frequency: 1-2 per month typically; more when intake is rich

**Evidence:** Attleborough Raj day-2 post (Aug 3), Butt Lane Hero — both fit the "concrete transformation quote in the first line" pattern that case study hero data proves works.

## PART 7 — Advocacy-with-action (the gap we own)

**The highest-value action-driving content on the page. Zero vendor competition.**

Data:
- EPS auto-renomination petition single-image = **597 clicks** (top single-link post ever)
- Bulk nomination petition (email version) = 218 clicks
- GTIN safety petition = 111 clicks

**When we ship:** any live sector issue where Titan takes a position (petition available, GPhC change, funding cut, patient choice threat, policy shift). Cam-led production, Wahid/Tariq sign-off on framing.

**Frequency:** 1-2 per month, driven by sector timing not calendar.

**Why this matters strategically:** every advocacy post is proof that Titan is on the pharmacist's side. PharmacyX/Cegedim/Positive/RxWeb/Apotec all fail this test. This is the position we own and the vendor set has vacated.

## PART 8 — Events playbook (data-grounded, retrospective + Q4 plan)

### Small events (regional CPE conferences, AGMs)
4-5 posts across 2 weeks. See-you-at + 1-2 live + named-people wrap.
**Devon / Leicestershire / Manchester CPGM tested — link clicks 2-5 per post. Don't over-invest.**

### Big events — Pharmacy Show 2025 retrospective (Oct 11-12 2025)

**What worked (data):**
- Day-after wrap post (Oct 14, "What a weekend") — **3,185 imp / 102 reactions / 3.61% ER** — peak post of the window
- Post-event single speaker video (Oct 16, Tariq talk) — 4,632 imp / 89 reactions
- Day-of "come chat with Sajid" video — 1,106 imp / 34 reactions / 3.16% ER (high for day-of solo)
- Named-Zad wrap (Oct 13) — 2,298 imp / 70 reactions

**What didn't:**
- Pre-event countdown videos (Oct 7, 9) — 542, 922 imp — modest reach for the effort
- Post-event carousel (Oct 21 "Inside Tariq's Talk") — 1,651 imp / 1.88% ER — video version outperformed doc version 3-to-1
- Long-tail case studies from show (Oct 22-23) — modest 1,300-1,500 imp

**Q4 Pharmacy Show 2026 playbook (October):**
- T-4 weeks: booth reveal + speaker preview (1 video, 1 image)
- T-2 weeks: "come find us at stand X" + guest speaker teaser
- T-1 week: 5/3/2 day countdown (T-3 countdown video hit 3.69% ER in 2025 — retain)
- **During:** 2-3 posts per day floor coverage + Sajid-style "come chat" clips
- **T+1-3 days:** peak slot — day-after wrap multi-image + individual speaker cutdowns as VIDEO (not documents — video cutdowns hit 2-3x doc engagement in 2025)
- T+1-3 weeks: named-people cutdowns respecting 3-cut rule per person

Improvement: cut generic pre-event Titan-branded countdown volume; invest in named-speaker teasers instead (personal-named content built better anticipation than branded).

### Owned events — TitanUp 2026 retrospective (June 7 2026, 300+ owners)

**What worked:**
- Day-of multi-image (June 7) — 3,809 imp / 67 reactions
- Day-after "What. A. Day." video (June 8) — 1,987 / 66 / 4.13% ER
- Post-event multi-image with specific pharmacy stat (June 23 Khal) — 3,988 / 69 reactions
- Named-guest speaker cuts (Rahul Gogna reveal) — 1,270 / 20 / 13.23% ER

**What didn't:**
- Over-saturated pre-event ramp (23+ posts May 15-June 6, mostly 500-1000 imp)
- Document format during event window (June 16 doc — 371 imp)
- Late-tail cutdowns (June 22-28 — mostly 800-1000 imp)

**Improvement for TitanUp 2027** (analyse to learn, not to plan yet): cut pre-event volume ~40%, keep day-of + day-after saturation (that's the peak window), compress post-event tail to 2 weeks respecting 3-cut rule, multi-image with specific pharmacy stats (June 23, 26 both 3,000+ imp).

**Big takeaway across both:** peak post is always the DAY-AFTER named-people wrap-up (multi-image or video), not the pre-event ramp.
