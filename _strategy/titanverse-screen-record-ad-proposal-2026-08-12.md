## 2. What this actually is

## 3. Three ad-content angles

Because the recording is third-party IP, none of these use the raw recording as the ad. Two of them rebuild the mechanic in Titanverse brand; one uses the recording as a citation inside a reaction piece. Trade-offs explained per angle.

---

### Angle A — Signature remake: "Unlock your ___" in Titanverse

**Visual treatment.** Full rebuild of Pavlo's exact mechanic in Titanverse brand. Cosmic-magenta radial bloom on cosmic-black. Same thin-serif type. Same word-swap rhythm on the italic. Same 3D metallic transition card resolving into the Titan mark. No frame of the source recording is used — the mechanic is what carries over, not the pixels. Built in Cavalry.

**On-screen hook line (4-sec title overlay, TikTok crop).**
*"Every new service adds four things. Are you ready for all of them?"*

**LinkedIn caption (Titanverse voice).**

Community pharmacy is becoming a services business.

Weight loss. Pharmacy First. Independent prescribing. Flu. Diagnostics. Every new service adds more consultations, more paperwork, more responsibility, more risk. Most pharmacies have added the services. Very few have changed the operating model underneath.

That's where good pharmacies get exposed.

The next pharmacy winners won't offer more services. They'll run them better.

More services shouldn't mean more chaos.

#Titanverse #CommunityPharmacy #PharmacyServices

**Format + platform.** LinkedIn native video (16:9 or 4:5), cross-post as IG Reel + YT Short in 9:16.
**Duration.** 15 seconds. The Pavlo original is 12 seconds of actual animation; the mechanic works because it holds each word beat long enough to read and short enough to stay taut. 15s gives room for a four-word cycle (Consultations / Paperwork / Responsibility / Risk) resolving on the Titanverse mark.

### Angle B — Reaction teardown: "This is what pharmacy marketing is missing"

**Visual treatment.** Split-screen or picture-in-picture. Left side: the recording playing (heavily cropped to remove Cam's personal LinkedIn UI, page names, and the Bravura Digital promoted post — see red flags). Right side: Cam or Hooman talking to camera. On-screen pointer + circle annotations at three moments: the bloom, the word-swap, the logo resolve. Cut with kinetic captions.

**On-screen hook line.**
*"An ad for a SaaS product has more clarity than most pharmacy websites. Here's why."*

**LinkedIn caption (Titanverse voice).**

This ad for a design tool made its point in twelve seconds. Most pharmacy software marketing takes twelve slides and still doesn't get there.

Three things it does that pharmacy marketing rarely does. It picks one variable and changes it. It holds each beat long enough to land. It resolves on the product, not on a feature list.

Community pharmacy is becoming a services business. The pitch for what runs those services should be at least as clear as the pitch for a website builder.

More services shouldn't mean more chaos.

#Titanverse #CommunityPharmacy

**Format + platform.** LinkedIn video first (this is a marketing-craft take, LI is the audience). Cutdown for IG Reel optional. Skip TikTok — off-audience.
**Duration.** 45 seconds. The teardown needs three annotated beats plus a reframe. Under 30s and it feels like a hot take; over 60s and it loses the audience before the reframe lands.

---

### Angle C — Are You Ready? animated signature (series glue)

**Visual treatment.** The strongest strategic use of the mechanic. Rebuild the cycling-word grammar as the **animated signature for the entire "Are You Ready?" arc**. Six word-cycles, one per episode topic, resolving on the Titanverse mark and the phrase *"Are you ready?"*.

Sequence: magenta bloom → *"Are you ready for"* → **weight loss / Pharmacy First / IP / flu / diagnostics** cycling on the italic → resolves into the Titanverse mark with the sign-off *"The operating layer for pharmacy services"*.

Ships as a standalone teaser 72 hours before the Manifesto carousel. Then the same signature (or 3–5 second cut-ins of it) opens or closes every carousel-supporting video across the arc, making the series visually recognisable across a scroll.

**On-screen hook line.**
*"Are you ready for what's coming?"*

**LinkedIn caption (Titanverse voice).**

Six services are about to reshape what a community pharmacy is expected to do. Weight loss. Pharmacy First. Independent prescribing. Flu at scale. Private diagnostics. A services business, whether the sector calls it that or not.

Over the next twelve weeks we're publishing one piece on each. Same question every time. Is your pharmacy ready?

Starts Wednesday.

More services shouldn't mean more chaos.

#Titanverse #CommunityPharmacy #PharmacyServices

**Format + platform.** LinkedIn native video (primary), 4:5 for feed weight. 9:16 cutdown for IG Reels + YT Shorts. TikTok skip.
**Duration.** 15 seconds for the teaser. 3-second cut-ins for reuse across the arc's supporting videos.

## 4. Recommended pick

**Angle C — Are You Ready? animated signature.** It does two jobs at once: it lands the mechanic on a piece that actually needs it (a series that already cycles through six services), and it produces reusable brand furniture that carries across the whole arc rather than a single ad.

## 5. Series fit

**Series glue for the "Are You Ready?" arc.** Angle C is the signature open/close for the Manifesto (Wed 19 Aug) and every episode after. Ship the teaser cut Mon 17 Aug to prime the Manifesto drop two days later.

Angle A works as a **standalone Titanverse video** dropping any time. Angle B is a **founder-led craft/marketing post** — Titanverse-branded but off the "Are You Ready?" spine, so runs adjacent to the series, not inside it. Neither belongs on Titan PMR — this is worldview and services stack, not dispensing operations.

---

## Red flags

- **Third-party IP.** The recording captures Pavlo Paniv's motion-design showcase for Framer. It cannot be republished. All three angles either rebuild the mechanic in Titanverse brand or use the recording as citation inside commentary. Angle B is the only one that puts source frames on screen; those frames need cropping to the animation area only, and even then we should credit Pavlo Paniv on-screen and in the caption to stay on the right side of fair use.
- **Personal + client account UI visible.** Cam's LinkedIn profile (name, photo, headline, page list including TITAN PMR and TITANVERSE, follower/impression counters), plus a Bravura Digital promoted post and a "Who Actually Owns Your Health Data?" YouTube card, are all in-frame in the pre-play and post-play seconds. Any use of those seconds needs a hard crop to the video player or a full mask of the sidebar and adjacent posts.
- **Video quality is fine, on-screen text is legible.** No technical blocker there.
- **No sensitive data.** No patient info, no client-confidential material, no financials. The only redaction pressure is UI cleanliness, not compliance.
